Last Content Update: 3 weeks ago

Please also review our Application Types and How to Submit guide (PDF) for clarification about which applications should be submitted on the Citizen Portal or via email.

If the type of permit you are submitting is not listed below, the permit application and supporting documents should be emailed to Planning & Zoning.

Land Disturbance and Floodplain Applications

There are three types of Land Disturbance Permit: Notice of Intent, Grading Permit, and Natural Surface Trail. The type of permit that applies depends on the total area of land disturbance and the nature of the work proposed. See Section 16.B for criteria. If you are unsure which permit applies, please use the Do I need a Land Disturbance Permit Worksheet or email us. 

  1. Grading Permit

The Grading Permit process applies to land disturbance that exceeds ½ acre. A Grading Permit undergoes a formal County review process with a referral to internal and external reviewers. All plans and reports shall bear the signature and seal of a registered professional engineer.   

Submittal Requirements for a Grading Permit: 

  • Cover Letter (narrative describing the proposed work) - Mandatory 
  • Completed and Signed Application Form - Mandatory 
  • A Grading Erosion and Sediment Control (GESC) Plan in conformance with Section 16 of the Zoning Resolution - Mandatory 
  • If there are geologic hazards or steep slopes proposed, a Geologic and/or Soils Investigation Report in conformance with Section 16 of the Zoning Resolution - Mandatory
  • A Drainage Report in conformance with the Storm Drainage Design and Technical Criteria - Mandatory
  • A quantity estimate (Exhibit A) for BMP's - Mandatory
  • Current Deed - Mandatory
  • If there will be any retaining walls taller than 3 feet, details and supporting calculations

If you have these documents ready to go, Click "Apply Now" below and you will be taken to the Online Application system.


Apply Now

  1. Notice of Intent

The Notice of Intent process applies to land disturbance that does not exceed ½ acre for a new start building permit for a primary structure (residential). The County relies on your Professional Engineer to prepare the Plans in accordance with County regulations and to certify through the N-1 Form that the Plans comply with County standards.

Note: This Notice of Intent process shall only apply to permits that meet the regulatory requirements related to grading, drainage and circulation; if any relief from standards is needed, a Grading Permit is required.

Submittal Requirements for a Notice of Intent: 

  • Cover Letter (narrative describing the proposed work) - Mandatory 
  • Completed and Signed Application form - Mandatory 
  • Completed and signed N-1 Form - Mandatory 
  • A Grading Erosion and Sediment Control (GESC) Plan in conformance with Section 16 of the Zoning Resolution - Mandatory 
  • A Drainage Report in conformance with the Storm Drainage Design and Technical Criteria - Mandatory 
  • Current Deed - Mandatory 
  • If there will be any retaining walls taller than 3 feet, you will need to apply for a separate Miscellaneous Permit 

If you have these documents ready to go, Click "Apply Now" below and you will be taken to the Online Application system.

  1. Natural Surface Trail

A Natural Surface Trail Permit is required for work involving the creation of natural surface trails, such as hiking or mountain biking trails. However, land disturbance for natural surface trails that disturb less than one acre are exempt. Land disturbance over one acre associated with the construction of natural surface trails shall follow the procedure outlined in the land disturbance section of the Zoning Resolution prior to commencement of any trail construction. 

Submittal Requirements for a Natural Surface Trail: 

  • Cover Letter - Mandatory
  • Natural Surface Trail Application Form - Mandatory 
  • Plans Showing Scope of Project and Construction Phasing - Mandatory 
  • Construction Guide that Includes Erosion and Sediment Control Measures - Mandatory 
  • Current Deed - Mandatory

If you have these documents ready to go, Click "Apply Now" below and you will be taken to the Online Application system.


Apply Now

  1. Floodplain Permit

Per Section 37 of the Zoning Resolution (ZR), any work in a floodplain requires a Floodplain Development Permit. There are two types of Floodplain Development Permits: Major and Minor. The type of permit that applies depends on the floodplain designation and the nature of the work proposed. See Section 37.H for criteria. 

Submittal Requirements for a Floodplain Permit (Minor): 

  • Cover Letter (narrative describing the proposed work) - Mandatory 
  • Completed and Signed Application Form (PDF) - Mandatory 
  • Current Deed - Mandatory 
  • A Site Plan that that follows the format described in ZR Section 37.H - Mandatory 
  • If the work will include improvements to an existing building, the assessed value of the existing building and a cost estimate of the proposed work. 

Submittal Requirements for a Floodplain Permit (Major): 

  • Cover Letter (narrative describing the proposed work) - Mandatory 
  • Completed and Signed Application Form - Mandatory 
  • Current Deed - Mandatory 
  • A Site Plan that that follows the format described in ZR Section 37.I.2 and includes Grading, Erosion and Sediment Control details as described in ZR Section 16.E - Mandatory 
  • A Floodplain Study that follows the format described in ZR Section 37.I.2 - Mandatory 
  • If the work will include improvements to an existing building, the assessed value of the existing building and a cost estimate of the proposed work. 

To assist the applicant with the Floodplain Permit Application process, Staff created 3 standard forms:

  • No-Adverse Impact
  • No-Rise Certification 
  • Manufactured Home in Floodplain 

If you have these documents ready to go, Click "Apply Now" below and you will be taken to the Online Application system.

Development Review Permit Applications

  1. Preliminary and Final Plat

Subdivision of land is handled through a Preliminary and Final Plat process. The resulting lots must meet the minimum lot size for the Zone District (see “What is My Zoning / What Can I Do on My Property?”), as well as other requirements such as Lot and Tract Standards and Public Health requirements for septic systems. 

General questions about specific properties should be directed to Planning Engineering Staff. You may also schedule an appointment with an engineer through our website.

Staff recommends applicants ready to move forward with a Preliminary and Final Plat schedule a Pre-Application Meeting prior to submitting a subdivision application. This meeting allows applicants to discuss their plans with Staff and receive preliminary feedback and guidance from Staff and External Agencies prior to formal application. 

Submittal Requirements for a Preliminary and Final Plat

See the Submittal Requirements Section of the Land Development Regulation for detailed information on each of these items. 

  • Cover Letter - Mandatory 
  • Application Form - Mandatory 
  • Wastewater Collection Information - Mandatory 
  • Water Supply Information - Mandatory 
  • Geologic and Geotechnical Report - Mandatory 
  • Environmental Questionnaire/Assessment - Mandatory 
  • Phase III Drainage Report - Mandatory 
  • Sensory Impact Assessment/Report/Plan - Mandatory 
  • Digital Survey Information - Mandatory 
  • Title Insurance Commitment - Mandatory 
  • Wildlife and Vegetation Assessment - Mandatory 
  • Utility Report/Plan - Mandatory 
  • Proof of Fire Protection - Mandatory 
  • Final Plat Document - Mandatory 
  • Supplemental Information - Mandatory
  • Boundary Closure Sheets - Mandatory 
  • Historical, Archaeological, and Paleontological Report/Plan - Mandatory 

May be Mandatory Depending on the Application:

  • Access Information
  • Wildfire Hazard Mitigation Plan
  • Construction Plans
  • Exhibit A
  • Transportation Information
  • Deeds/Easements/Agreements
  • Radiation Assessment/Report/Plan
  • Parking Plan
  • Landscape Plan
  • Vegetation Preservation Plan
  • Mineral Estate Notification Form

If you have the mandatory documents ready to go and have spoken with Staff about your application, Click "Apply Now" below and you will be taken to the Online Application system.

Minor Adjustment Applications

The Minor Adjustment Process may be used for a variety of development activities as described in Section 11.B of the Land Development Regulation. Depending on the Minor Adjustment process being applied for there is one major difference which is the requirement for the submittal and review of a survey.  

  1. Minor Adjustment (Survey)

The Minor Adjustment with Survey may be used to reconfigure or eliminate interior lot lines, create commercial superlots, adjust previously established building envelopes, non-buildable areas or non-disturbance areas, create lot lines to convert a duplex to a single family attached or to create a non-buildable tract.  

Submittal Requirements for a Minor Adjustment with Survey

  • A Minor Adjustment Application Form (PDF) - Mandatory 
  • A Cover Letter describing your plans and including contact information - Mandatory 
  • Current Deed and Pre-2000 Deed (For metes and bounds parcels) - Mandatory 
  • An Ownership & Encumbrances Report or Title Insurance Commitment for each lot - Mandatory 
  • If the owner is a LLC, Corporation, Trust or other non-individual entity, additional documents will be needed to confirm signature authority of whoever will be signing 
  • A Minor Adjustment Document in the format described in Section 8 of the Land Development Regulation (PDF) - Mandatory 
  • Water Supply Information: a will-serve letter from the water district or a well permit. Well permits may be obtained through the Colorado Division of Water Resources - Mandatory 
  • If existing or resulting lots will use a septic system, Form 6002 from Public Health's web page
  • If land will be exchanged between two owners, a draft deed for property transfer. This can be prepared by a land use attorney

If you have these documents ready to go, Click "Apply Now" below and you will be taken to the Online Application system.


Apply Now

  1. Minor Adjustment (No Survey)

The  Minor Adjustment without Survey may be used to eliminate building envelopes, adjust previously approved development agreements, and for changes to or elimination of notes, restrictions and certificates. 

Submittal Requirements for a Minor Adjustment (No Survey)

  • A Minor Adjustment Application - Mandatory 
  • A Cover Letter describing your plans and including contact information - Mandatory 
  • If the owner is a LLC, Corporation, Trust or other non-individual entity, additional documents will be needed to confirm signature authority of whoever will be signing

If you have these documents ready to go, Click "Apply Now" below and you will be taken to the Online Application system.
