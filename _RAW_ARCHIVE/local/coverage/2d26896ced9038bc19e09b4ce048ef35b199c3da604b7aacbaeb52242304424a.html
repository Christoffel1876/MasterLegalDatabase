Local Government Information System (LGIS)

Welcome to the Department of Local Affairs’ Local Government Information System (LGIS).

This LGIS is intended for individuals seeking information about local governments in Colorado including contact information, budget filings, and election filings.

Please note:

The information within the LGIS is based on filings from local governments to the Division of Local Government (DLG).

The Department of Local Affairs does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of the local government information provided herein.

The information provided through the LGIS does not constitute legal, financial, or professional advice. Users should consult with qualified professionals for specific advice.

LGIS is intended for interactive use by individuals. Malicious or disruptive use that could impair the system's functionality or availability for other users is prohibited.

If you are unable to access any portion of these documents, please visit the DOLA Digital Accessibility Accommodations or Modifications page or use the ADA Accessibility Accommodation Digital Service Request form.

Your use of this system indicates your acknowledgment and acceptance of the above.

If you have any questions, please email DLG at dola_dlg_helpdesk@state.co.us.
