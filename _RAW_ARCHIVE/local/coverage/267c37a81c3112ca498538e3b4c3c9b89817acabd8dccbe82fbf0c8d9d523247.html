Building Permit Process

Who Needs a Building Permit?

Golden’s building permit process is intended to make sure that all buildings, whether they are for living or for business, meet certain safety and health standards.

Before you start any construction or changes to your building, including projects like enlarging, repairing, demolishing, or changing its use, or if you plan to work on any electrical, gas, mechanical, or plumbing systems, you need to get a permit from the City. This includes installing, altering, or fixing any of these systems.

Before you Apply

  1. Is the project address within the city limits of Golden? Go to the Jefferson County Address Wizard, enter the address, select the address you are researching, scroll to bottom of page for Municipal Status.
  2. If project address is not within city limits, but part of unincorporated Jefferson County, you will need to contact the Jefferson County Building Safety Division for instructions to proceed.
  3. If project is within city limits, visit our Required Permits Guide to see if you need a permit before proceeding with your project plans.
  4. Check the Wildland Urban Interface (WUI) map to see whether your property is located in a WUI area.

How to Apply

Once you have determined that a permit is necessary,  you will be able to do the following in the online portal. 

  • Apply for a permit under “Building”
  • Apply for a Contractor License (required for all contractors working on a permitted project within Golden city limits)
  • Request an inspection
  • If your project includes adding square footage or performing any exterior work, additional permits may be required by the Planning Department. Review zoning requirements on our Zoning Information page.

*Please Note: All licensed contractors must be identified on your building permit BEFORE the permit can be issued. Building permits are inclusive of all trades.

Register for an account on our Online Permitting Portal.

Permit Fees & Building Use Tax

Please use the Building Permit Fee Table to determine the permit fees based on your valuation. Valuation is the cost of the labor and materials. For homeowners performing their own work, the valuation is calculated as materials times two. In the City of Golden, all construction projects requiring a building permit are subject to a building use tax. The building permit fee includes a prepayment of this use tax, based on your project's estimated costs. For more information, visit our Taxes & Fees page.

All records requests related to historical building permits are completed through a Public Records Request.

Roofing and Re-Roof Covering Requirements

This is a summary of Golden's roofing requirements--NOT a comprehensive list. Applicants are responsible for following all applicable codes and ordinances.

If you are part of a Homeowners Association (HOA), please consult with them before applying for a roofing permit as additional regulations may apply. 

Important Notice:
As the permit holder, you must provide access for both mid-roof and final inspections. Mid-roof inspections are required when 25% to 65% of the new roof covering has been installed, and a ladder must be secured and extended to at least three feet above the roofline. Failure to provide access may result in reinspection fees. If a mid-roof inspection is missed, two squares of roofing must be removed for inspection.

Key Roofing Requirements:

  • Wind Speed: The basic wind speed requirement is 150 mph Vult / 116 mph Vasd, with a 3-second gust in exposure C conditions.
  • Snow Load: The minimum roof snow load is 30 pounds per square foot.
  • Ice Barrier: Golden requires an approved ice barrier for all new and re-roofing applications. This barrier must extend from the lowest edge to a point no less than 24 inches inside the exterior wall line of the building. IRC 2024 section R905.1.2 and IBC 2024 section 1507.1.2
  • Drip Edge: Drip edges are required for all new and re-roofing applications at both eaves and rake edges. Installation must follow IRC 2021 Section R905.2.8.5 and IBC 2021 Section 1507.2.9.3 standards.
  • Fire Rating: Wood shakes and shingles must meet a minimum Class A fire rating. All other roofing materials must have at least a Class C fire rating.
  • Roof Ventilation: Proper roof and attic ventilation must comply with IRC 2021 Section R806 and manufacturer guidelines.
  • Metal Flashing: Damaged or rusted metal flashing must be replaced.

Additional requirements include keeping siding or wall finishes 2 to 4 inches above the roof/wall junction on lower roofs, ensuring commercial ballasted roofs undergo an engineering evaluation for load capacity, and meeting structural roof component standards per IEBC 2021 Section 1501.2.1.

Re-Roofing and Repairs:

If a repair exceeds 25% of the total roof area, the entire roof must be replaced. Roof replacement shall include the removal of all existing layers of roof coverings down to the roof deck. IRC 2024 R908.3 and IBC 2024 1512.2

Asphalt Shingle Roofing Requirements:

  1. Only one overlay is allowed on existing asphalt or wood shingles. T-Lock shingles cannot be overlaid. The total weight of the combined roof layers must not exceed 750 pounds per square unless verified by a structural analysis.
  2. Fasteners must be corrosion-resistant, long enough to penetrate at least ¾" into the decking, and comply with ASTM F1667 standards. Staples are not permitted.
  3. Shingles must have a minimum weight of 300 pounds per square or a 30-year manufacturer’s warranty.
  4. Asphalt shingles can only be used on roofs with a slope of at least 17% or 2:12.
  5. Shingles must be fastened with at least six nails per shingle and be self-sealing or hand-sealed.
  6. A starter course with adhesive is required at the eave line.
  7. For complete tear-offs, a minimum Type 15 asphalt-saturated felt underlayment must be installed.
  8. For low-slope roofs: For roof slopes from two units vertical in 12 units horizontal (17-percent slope) up to four units vertical in 12 units horizontal (33-percent slope), double underlayment application is required in accordance with IRC 2024 table R905.1.1.(2) and IBC 2024 table 1507.1.1(2). Starting at the eave apply 36-inch-wide sheet.

For more details, contact the City of Golden Building Department.

Plan Review Process

The City of Golden recognizes the need for timely plan reviews to help customers manage construction schedules, financing, and budgeting, while also ensuring we can allocate resources efficiently.

Our primary focus, however, is on providing thorough and accurate reviews, as accuracy should not be sacrificed for speed. Because projects vary in size and complexity, it's impractical to set uniform review times, and factors such as staffing levels, workloads, and incomplete submissions can impact turnaround times.

Please Note:

  • Review comments will only be sent to the contact person listed on the permit application.
  • Once plan review comments are provided, all responses must be resubmitted at one time to all relevant agencies through the building division.

Targeted Plan Review Timeframes

This policy sets reasonable goals for plan review periods, applying to the initial review only. Subsequent reviews or resubmissions may require additional time.

Review Periods
  • Residential Permits that require a Plan Review:  15 working days
  • Interior Commercial Alterations: 5 to 15 working days (1-3 weeks)
  • Commercial/Multi-Family Additions: 10 to 20 working days (2-4 weeks)
  • New Single-Family Homes: 20 to 25 working days (3-5 weeks)
  • New Commercial/Multi-Family Projects: 20 to 30 working days (4-6 weeks)

Please Note:

  • These timeframes are goals, not guarantees. Larger or more complex projects may require more time.
  • Additional time may be needed for incomplete, inaccurate, or improperly submitted plans, specifications, or applications.

For questions or to submit your plans, please contact the City of Golden Building Department.

Demolition Permit Requirements

When applying for a demolition permit, additional documents and conditions may be required by the Building Official. These may include, but are not limited to:

  1. Demolition Plans: Detailed plans and specifications outlining the extent of the demolition work.
  2. Demolition Schedule: A complete timeline for the demolition process.
  3. Utility Discontinuance: Verification that utilities such as water, sewer, electric, and natural gas have been properly disconnected according to the utility provider’s guidelines. Water and sewer lines must be disconnected and capped at the tie-in point to the city mains and inspected by the City of Golden Public Works before backfilling. Right-of-way permits may also be required.
  4. Structural Integrity Plans: Structural designs, calculations, and engineering may be required for any shoring or bracing necessary to maintain the safety and integrity of remaining structures. (Note: Excavations within 10 feet of adjacent structures, where the slope from the property line exceeds 45 degrees, will require a shoring plan prepared by a licensed engineer in Colorado.)
  5. Future Plans: Conceptual drawings for future remodeling, redevelopment, or change of use related to the demolition.
  6. Trash and Material Storage: Approval from the Planning and Development Department, Fire Department, and Public Works/Right-of-Way for the placement of trash containers or enclosures for demolition materials, especially if they are in the city right-of-way.
  7. Asbestos Abatement: Documentation of asbestos-containing material (ACM) removal and an Air Clearance Report from the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment.

City Ordinances to Keep in Mind:

  • 5.15.020 (Municipal Code) - Sound Restrictions:
    No activity, vehicle, machine, or device may produce sound that exceeds the decibel limits specified by zone:

    • Daytime (7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.)
    • Nighttime (10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.)

  • 5.01.070 (Municipal Code) - Nuisance Control:

    • Dust Control: It is unlawful to allow dust from construction or traffic areas without implementing control measures, such as wetting down affected areas.
    • Open Excavations: Any excavation over five feet deep, including wells or cisterns, must be covered or enclosed by a fence or barricade as deemed appropriate by the Public Works Director.
    • Street Cleanliness: It is unlawful to track or deposit mud, dirt, or debris on public roads or rights-of-way from construction activities.

  • 13.30.030 (Municipal Code) - Erosion Control:
    Activities disturbing land surfaces must control soil erosion, sedimentation, and water flow changes to prevent pollution of water systems.

  • 13.30.060 (Municipal Code) - Stormwater Quality Permits:
    If your activity disturbs more than half an acre of land, you must obtain a Stormwater Quality Permit from the city. For smaller areas, appropriate measures must be taken to prevent sediment and debris from entering storm drains, gutters, and streets.

For more information, contact the City of Golden's Building Department or review the relevant city ordinances.

Inspection Guidelines

Once a construction permit has been issued, it is the responsibility of the contractor or homeowner to ensure that the building is ready for inspection on the scheduled date.

Policy and Procedure for Scheduling Inspections

  • Current Permits: Inspections must be scheduled through the user’s ACA Citizens Portal account.
  • Historical Permits: Inspections should be called into the inspection request line at (303) 384-8198 by 4:00 p.m. on the previous business day. Please provide the permit number, address, type of inspection requested, and any special conditions.

Types of Inspections

Footing or Caisson Inspection:
  • Caissons must be inspected by a licensed Professional Engineer in Colorado, and a stamped inspection report must be submitted.
  • Footing inspections should be requested after excavation is completed and the forms and rebar are in place. These can be performed by the City Inspector or a professional engineer if prior arrangements are made. A signed and stamped report must be provided to the city before the foundation and damp-proofing inspection.
Concrete Reinforcing Inspection:
  • This inspection ensures the forms and reinforcements for foundation walls or grade beams are ready for the concrete pour.
  • It follows the same procedure as the footing or caisson inspection.
Foundation and Damp Proofing Inspection:
  • Before backfilling, all defects must be grouted, and damp-proofing applied to areas below the backfill.
  • This inspection must occur before backfill.
Combination Inspections:
  • The City of Golden conducts "combination" inspections to streamline resources.
  • All trades associated with the project should complete their work before requesting an inspection that covers multiple facets.
Rough Plumbing Inspection:
  • This inspection is requested after plumbing pipes are installed.
  • The underground plumbing, drains, vents, and water piping can be inspected together or separately.
  • Drain, waste, and vent systems require a 5 psi pressure test or 10 feet of water head.
  • Water piping must be under street pressure or tested at 50 psi.
Gas Pipe Inspection:
  • A gas line inspection with a 10 psi air test must be requested before gas lines are concealed.
  • Both this inspection and the meter release inspection are required before the gas meter is installed.
Water Heater Inspection:
  • A final plumbing inspection is required after installing all necessary equipment.
  • Thermal expansion tanks must be installed according to the manufacturer’s guidelines and labeled with the set pressure.
Rough Heating Inspection:
  • This inspection is requested once all ductwork, flues, heating, hot water, and refrigeration lines are installed but not yet covered or concealed.
Rough Electrical Inspection:
  • Requested after all outlet boxes (switches, lights, receptacles) are installed, the wiring is pulled, and connections are made.
  • Underground electrical inspections may also be required.
Heat Meter Inspection:
  • Can be performed during the rough electrical inspection if the heating appliance is installed and operational and the main electrical panel is completed.
Rough Frame Inspection:
  • Before requesting this inspection, ensure that:
    • Interior partitions, framing, sub-flooring, and stairs are completed and ready to be covered.
    • Any damage or alterations made by other trades are repaired and meet code requirements.
    • Rough plumbing, heating, and electrical inspections have been completed.
    • The construction complies with the approved plans.
Insulation Inspection:
  • This inspection is conducted after the rough frame inspection and once insulation is installed.
Drywall Inspection:
  • Performed after drywall is fully attached to the framing but before taping or covering.
Final Plumbing, Heating, and Electrical Inspections:
  • These can be requested in any order once all related work is complete.
  • Ensure the appropriate utility meters are installed before requesting final inspections.

For more details or to schedule an inspection, visit the City of Golden’s ACA Citizens Portal or call the inspection request line for historical permits.

Setback/Height Survey Verification Form

The Setback/Height Survey Verification Form must be completed by a professional land surveyor licensed in the State of Colorado.

If you are having an engineer inspect and approve the footing/foundation and there is a setback survey requirement on the permit, the engineer's letter will NOT be accepted until the survey has been received and approved.

Residential Generators and Energy Storage Systems (ESS)
Residential Generator
  • An electrical permit is required.
  • Provide a site plan outlining the gas line for the generator location.
  • Provide the manufacturer specifications of the generator.
  • Provide the fuel gas source of the generator ( natural gas, propane, diesel)
Residential Energy Storage System
  • An electrical permit is required.
  • Provide a site plan or floor plan of the location of the ESS.
  • Manufacturer specifications
Commercial Emergency Generator
  • Apply for a Commercial Miscellaneous permit
  • Provide a site plan of the location.
  • Follow Fire Department and Building Department guidelines.
  • Provide detailed drawings for a complete Plan Review by staff.

All projects must meet the following structural design criteria: 

  • Ground Snow Load:  

                    Pg = 69 psf (Risk Category II)

                    Pg = 76 psf (Risk Category III)

                    Pg = 83 psf (Risk Category IV)

  • Wind Load: 

                    Vult = 150 mph (Risk Category II)

                    Vult = 157 mph (Risk Category III)

                    Vult = 164 mph (Risk Category IV)
       *** Exposure C

  • Frost Depth:  36 inches
Golden Wildfire Resiliency Code (GWRC),
The City of Golden has adopted the Golden Wildfire Resiliency Code (GWRC), established by Ordinance No. 2290 and codified as Chapter 15.80 of the Golden Municipal Code, to protect lives and property from the threat of wildfire in our community.  The GWRC applies to new construction, significant additions, repairs, and remodels on properties located within Golden's designated Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) areas — as shown on the Official Golden Wildfire Map — and establishes fire-resistant building construction standards for two hazard classifications: Class I (low fire intensity) and Class II (medium and high fire intensity). The code takes effect for enforcement beginning June 10th, 2026, and copies are available for public inspection at the Golden Fire Department, 911 10th Street, Golden, CO 80401. For questions about whether your property is located within the WUI or how the code may affect your project, please contact the City of Golden Building Department at (303) 384-8151
Processes Related to Building Permits
Fire Hydrant Meter Application for Temporary Water Service

To use water from a Golden Fire Hydrant for construction purposes, submit a Fire Hydrant Meter Application for Temporary Water Service. Please email building@cityofgolden.net to request the application. 

 

Right of Way (ROW) Permits

ROW Permits are issued to allow construction work in the public right-of-way (ROW). These permits are also required for dumpsters, storage pods, storage of landscape equipment parking, and sidewalk lane closures.

Please visit our Right of Way Permits page for information on ROW permits and how to apply for one.

Bulk Water Station

During the amended Stage 1 drought order, the City may limit, restrict, or temporarily suspend commercial water use from the bulk water station as needed to protect Golden’s water supply and prioritize essential community needs.

The City of Golden bulk water sales station is located on the north end of Golden at 1151 Catamount Drive, adjacent to the City of Golden maintenance shops.

This station provides bulk water to residents and contractors without the customer needing to rent a hydrant meter. A standard 2.5″ male fire hose connection is installed on the station, so customers will need the appropriate adaptor for a connection.

The discharge from the station is currently set at approximately 250 gpm.

How to use the Bulk Water Station

  1. Customers must set up a prepaid account with the City of Golden. Accounts may be created by the Public Works staff.
  2. Complete the application and email to building@cityofgolden.gov for assistance in creating an account. Bulk Water applications and payments for adding water to the station account are best handled Monday - Thursday.
  3. Be prepared to provide company and truck information, including license plate numbers and truck capacity.
