Development Applications

APPLICATION PROCESS

  1. Zoning Identification / Due Diligence: Visit the Zoning Information Page. Review the zoning map and information for your property or project area.
  2. Coordinate Pre-Submittal Requirements: Check the review process for the specific situation or project. A pre-application meeting is not required in most cases and meeting with staff should not replace reading or understanding the code and requirements.
  3. Submit Application: Applications are submitted through the OpenGov Online Permitting Portal. For detailed instructions and requirements based on your project type, such as subdivisions, rezonings, PUD amendments, or other processes, please refer to the specific application pages below.

Application review process

  1. Intake for completeness and technical sufficiency (within 2-5 days). Payment per fee schedule. Review timelines depend largely on the quality and completeness of application materials, as well as the responsiveness to requests for resubmission or additional information. 
  2. First review comments based on staff and other agency reviews (Staff works to return comments in approximately 3-4 weeks of accepted, complete submittals)
  3. Applicant revisions and resubmittal (responsive to all corrections, comments, and questions. There are generally two to three rounds of staff review to identify corrections, clarifications, and opportunities to better demonstrate compliance with approval criteria)
  4. Resubmittal reviews (within 15 days depending on quality and completeness and staff capacity)
  5. Final determination or public hearing process

More Information

  • Planning Development Projects: View public hearing notices, recently approved projects, and new developments currently under construction.
  • Public Meetings page: View agendas for upcoming meetings, and archived meeting materials, including videos and minutes.
  • City Calendar: Get information on upcoming neighborhood meetings and community engagement opportunities.
