Environmental Health

The Environmental Health department helps protect Clear Creek County's public health and environment by preventing and addressing safety hazards. Please see the list of County Environmental Health services offered below, as well as information about several services available through the State of Colorado.

County Environmental Health Programs

  • Environmental Complaints
  • New Septic Construction and Repairs 
  • Septic Use Permits
  • Water Quality
  • Air Quality
  • Zoonotic Diseases Prevention (Rabies, Plague, etc)
  • Communicable Diseases

State Environmental Health Programs

  • Well Permitting
    This program is through the Colorado Division of Water Resources
  • State Retail Food Inspections
    This program is through the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment
  • School and Childcare Inspections
    This program is through the Colorado Department of Human Services
