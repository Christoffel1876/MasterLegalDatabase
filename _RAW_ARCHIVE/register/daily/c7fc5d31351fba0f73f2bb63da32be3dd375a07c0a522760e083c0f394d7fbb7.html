Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Office of Natural Medicine Licensure will hold a Rulemaking Hearing on Thursday, October 8, 2025, at 9:00 A.M., to receive testimony before the Director of the Division of Professions and Occupations (“Director”) decides whether to adopt proposed revisions to Rule 5.4 – Continuing Education Requirements. The purpose is to revise the current rules related to continuing education requirements for Natural Medicine Facilitators and Clinical Facilitators.
