Subjects and Issues Involved The proposed rules are responsive to new state legislation, a settlement agreement resulting from a state complaint filed against the Colorado Department of Education by Disability Justice (formerly Disability Law Colorado), and recent stakeholder input. They clarify Administrative Unit (AU) responsibilities for determining whether a facility school placement constitutes a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE) in the Least Restrictive Environment (LRE) prior to any placement, establish required timelines for AU action following public agency notifications or emergency placements, strengthen expectations for AU monitoring of FAPE and LRE during a facility placement, formalize discharge and exit procedures, and refine residency, public placement, and reimbursement definitions and processes. The proposed amendments also clarify the roles of the Colorado School for the Deaf and the Blind (CSDB) and districts of residence and establish explicit timelines for district responses to parent evaluation requests. Collectively, these proposed revisions improve statewide consistency, reinforce statutory and IDEA alignment, and provide clearer operational guidance for AUs, facility schools, and public agencies.
Statutory Authority These rules implement the Exceptional Children's Educational Act (ECEA), § 22-20-101 et seq., C.R.S., under the rulemaking authority granted in §§ 22-20-104, 22-20-108(4), 22-20-114(1)(a)(I)(A)–(C), and 22-20-123(3)(b), C.R.S.
