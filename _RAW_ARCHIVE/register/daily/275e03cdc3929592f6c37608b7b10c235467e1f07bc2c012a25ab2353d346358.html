Subjects and Issues Involved By this Decision, the Colorado Public Utilities Commission (“Commission”) issues this Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (“NOPR”) to consider amendments and additions to the Rules Regulating Electric Utilities, 4 Code of Colorado Regulations (“CCR”) 723-3. Through this NOPR, the Commission seeks to codify minimum standards for utility wildfire risk assessment, mitigation planning, and proactive de-energization, including through public safety power shutoffs (“PSPS”). More specifically, and as explained below, the Commission proposes two sections of related rules: one for utility Wildfire Mitigation Plans (“WMPs”) and a second for PSPS events.
Statutory Authority The statutory authority for the rules proposed here is found at §§ 40-3-103, and 40 6-109(1), C.R.S.
