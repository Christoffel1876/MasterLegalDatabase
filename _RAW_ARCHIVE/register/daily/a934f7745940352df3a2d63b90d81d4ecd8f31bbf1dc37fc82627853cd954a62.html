Subjects and Issues Involved The proposed rule makes minor clarifying updates to the rules and is being heard to correct a clerical error. Rule 39-28.8-201. Retail Marijuana Sales Tax. The purpose of this rule is to include donations in the definition of a “transfer,” and to update the language in paragraph (3) of the rule to “wholesale transfers” instead of “wholesale sales.” Rule 39-28.8-301. Retail Marijuana Excise Tax. The purpose of this rule is to update certain definitions, make some clarifying updates to paragraphs (2), (3), and (5) of the rule, detail additional transitional rules for the calculation of the average market rate, and further explain recordkeeping requirements.
