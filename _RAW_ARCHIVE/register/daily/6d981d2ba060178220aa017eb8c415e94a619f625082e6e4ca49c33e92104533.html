Subjects and Issues Involved The Executive Director of the Colorado Department of Revenue, on behalf of the Firearms Dealer Division, will consider the promulgation of the Firearms Dealer Rules, 1 C.C.R 214-1, as authorized by Colorado Revised Statutes (“C.R.S.”), Part 4 of Article 12 of Title 18 ("Firearms - Dealers"). For specific information and language concerning the proposed changes and new rules, please refer to the contents of this Notice and the proposed rules that are set forth following this notice and are available on the following website: https://sbg.colorado.gov/2026-firearms-dealer-rulemaking-working-groups
Statutory Authority The Executive Director promulgates these rules pursuant to the authority granted in sections 18-12-401.5(2)(b), 18-12-406(5), of Part 4 of Article 12 of Title 18, C.R.S., and 24-4-103 and 24-4-106(11), C.R.S., of the Administrative Procedure Act, and the specific rulemaking authority set forth in the basis and purpose for each rule incorporated herein and identified below.
