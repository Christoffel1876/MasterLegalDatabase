Subjects and Issues Involved A 2025 Colorado Supreme Court (2025CO28, People in the Int. of B.C.B.) found that the definition of “affected by” in Volume 7 (“Social Services Rules”) is inconsistent with the plain meaning of the current statute CRS 19-1-103(1)(a)(IV). This rule change is necessary to align the definition used in Volume 7 with the Court’s legal interpretation of current statute.
