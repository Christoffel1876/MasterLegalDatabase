Subjects and Issues Involved This rule packet is necessary to update income calculations standards, incorporate current federal fiscal year income guidelines, and clarify how different types of income and application timeliness are handled regarding the Low-Income Energy Assistance Program (LEAP). These changes help ensure applications are reviewed consistently, fairly, and accurately for all applicants.
