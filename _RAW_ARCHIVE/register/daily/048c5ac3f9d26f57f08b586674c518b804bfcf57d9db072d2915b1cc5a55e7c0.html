Subjects and Issues Involved C.R.S. Section 43-2-150 now allows installation or removal of tire chains or alternate traction devices (ATDs) at a location designated in the permit on state highways. The law requires the Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT) to create rules to effectuate this new permit process. Accordingly, CDOT has updated the current chain rules. The updated rules: (a) contain the procedures for issuing and revoking a permit and the requirements to be issued a permit, including laying out permittee responsibilities; (b) include safety requirements inducing that the individuals installing tire chains or Alternate Traction Devices (ATD) must wear reflective clothing and use appropriate signs and traffic control devices; (c) clarify that Tire Cables as ATDs on Commercial Motor Vehicles are permitted on a tandem power drive axle combination with super single tires only if they have a chain on one axle and have cables or chains on the second axle; and (d) define Tow Tuck and Vehicle Recovery Unit and clarify that both are exempt from certain parts of these rules under certain circumstances.
Statutory Authority C.R.S. Section 43-2-150
