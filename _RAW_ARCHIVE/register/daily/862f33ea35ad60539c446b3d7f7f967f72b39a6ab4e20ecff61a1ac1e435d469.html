Subjects and Issues Involved Senate Bill 25-007 created the Prescribed Fire Claims Cash Fund in the Division of Fire Prevention and Control in the Colorado Department of Public Safety under the authority of Section 24-33.5-1240, C.R.S. In accordance with statute, the Division Director shall adopt rules to administer this cash fund and to implement the increased prescribed burning program requirements for certified burner reciprocity and activities as set forth in Sections 24-33.5-1202(2.) and 24-33.5-1217, C.R.S. The rules apply to all prescribed fire activities registered with the Division of Fire Prevention and Control (Division) and any claims submitted for damages arising from those activities.
Statutory Authority 24-33.5-1240, 24-33.5-1217, 24-33.5-1202(2), C.R.S.
