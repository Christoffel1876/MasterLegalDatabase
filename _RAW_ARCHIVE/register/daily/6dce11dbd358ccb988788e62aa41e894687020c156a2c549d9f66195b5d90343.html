Rule Title MOBILE HOME PARK OVERSIGHT PROGRAM

Rulemaking Hearing

Date 11/02/2026
Time 01:30 PM
Location 1570 Grant Street, Centennial Conference Room, Denver, CO 80203

Subjects and Issues Involved To implement and clarify the Mobile Home Park Act, Title 38, Article 12, Part 2 of the Colorado Revised Statutes (C.R.S.), and the Mobile Home Park Act Dispute Resolution and Enforcement Program, Title 38, Article 12, Part 11, C.R.S., pursuant to statutory authority and changes made through HB26-1224 Protections for Mobile Home Park Residents (effective January 1, 2027). Specific subjects and issues involved include the definition of "mobile home," requirements for responding to the division's subpoenas, park rules and regulations that landlords cannot enforce or cannot enforce without a specific basis for the rule, landlord requirements related to sewer line maintenance and sewage spills, and provisions related to the home owner opportunity to purchase law when a mobile home park is for sale.
Statutory Authority Pursuant to section 38-12-1104(2)(j), C.R.S.
