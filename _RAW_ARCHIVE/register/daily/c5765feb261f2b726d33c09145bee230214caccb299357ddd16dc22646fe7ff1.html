Subjects and Issues Involved This rule packet will incorporate requirements from HB24-1017 “Bill of Rights for Foster Youth” and its implementing statute in Section 19-7-101, C.R.S. The rule packet will also update 12 CCR 2509-8 Section 7.706, specifically regulations for Day Treatment Facilities, to be aligned with current statute, practice, and expectations, as well as newly adopted regulations in Section 7.701 as of July 1, 2025, where possible. This rule packet also contains technical updates to regulations that were published July 1, 2025, specific to grammar, spelling, capitalization, or other substantive errors made prior.
