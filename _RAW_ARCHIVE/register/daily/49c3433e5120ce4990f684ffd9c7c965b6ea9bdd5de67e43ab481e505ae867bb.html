Subjects and Issues Involved This rule packet aligns prioritized Chapters of the Colorado Behavioral Health Administration (BHA) provider rules with current national standards in the treatment and crisis continuum, and restructures the volume in support of a foundational Chapter 1 that addresses requirements applicable to all BHA-issued credentials. Colorado is statutorily required to build substance use disorder (SUD) services based on the most recent version of the American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM) Criteria, as found in Sections 10-16-104(5.5)(a), C.R.S., and 25.5-5-424, C.R.S. Additionally, Colorado is committed to full alignment with the Fourth Edition of the ASAM Criteria by July 1, 2027, to remain eligible for federal funding through the 1115 Demonstration Waiver managed by Health Care Policy and Financing (HCPF), pursuant to Section 25.5-5-424, C.R.S. This packet focuses on alignment with the Adult Volume of the 4th Edition of the ASAM Criteria. Treatment within the 4th Edition differs from current state standards, notably removing withdrawal management as a standalone service. As a result, this packet requires additional alignment with Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) crisis standards to ensure the behavioral health continuum is equipped to rapidly serve individuals experiencing behavioral health crises, including SUD-involved crises.
