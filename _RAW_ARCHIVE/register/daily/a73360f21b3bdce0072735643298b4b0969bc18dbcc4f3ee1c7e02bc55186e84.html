Subjects and Issues Involved In Part 1, establish definitions for terms introduced in House Bill 26-1289, refine existing terms, and remove terms that are no longer relevant. In Part 2, amend the total dollar amount the Department may award in any tax year. In Part 3, remove the section pertaining to tax credit letter of eligibility submission, review, and issuance, establish revised application prioritization and amend the Department’s time to review, approve, and deny applications. In Part 5, amend purchaser detail. In Part 6, amend the list of eligible and non-eligible equipment purchases.
Statutory Authority 39-22-549(4)(a), (5)(e), and (9), C.R.S
