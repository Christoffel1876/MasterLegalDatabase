Subjects and Issues Involved State law requires CDOT to conduct a review of its rules to assess the continuing need for, the appropriateness of, and the cost effectiveness of its rules to determine if they should be continued in their current form, modified, or repealed. Rules 2 CCR 601-18 have been in place since 2008 (updated in 2012). The rules purpose was to establish consistency regarding the applications for recycled waste tire grants pertaining to noise mitigation measures pursuant to sections 43-2-401 to 404 C.R.S The rules and noise mitigation statute are outdated. Because the rules are no longer needed, the mandate to make rules under statute was removed this legislative session as part of the CDOT statutory cleanup bill HB 26-1076. The rules were originally created to promote waste tires as material to build noise walls. But, no noise mitigation measures of any type have ever been built under the rules. If an applicant were to apply for a to build noise mitigation measure on CDOT right of way, the current statute provides enough information to process that request without the rules. For these reasons, CDOT through this rulemaking process, pending public comment, plans to repeal the rule.
Statutory Authority § 43-2-401 et seq. C.R.S.
