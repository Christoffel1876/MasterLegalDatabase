Subjects and Issues Involved HB25-1292 requires that CDOT update its rules concerning access to state highway right of way (ROW) to accommodate high voltage lines (also called transmission lines) pursuant to the state highway Utility Accommodation Code, 2 CCR 601-18, or any successor code. Accordingly, the Department has drafted edits to the Utility Accommodation Code. The rules redline clarifies as the law requires that longitudinal high voltage lines may be permitted in state highway Right of Way (ROW) if identified criteria are met. The updated rules also: Contain a process through which a transmission developer must submit a permit request to the Department for the use of a state highway ROW to construct a high voltage line. Establish the process for the denial of a permit request submitted by a transmission developer for a high voltage line if the proposed project presents a risk to public safety or prevents the proper functioning of the state highway. Set the surcharges for a transmission developer’s access to a state highway ROW.
