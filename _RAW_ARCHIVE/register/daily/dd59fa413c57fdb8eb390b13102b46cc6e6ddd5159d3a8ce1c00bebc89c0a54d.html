Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Office of Outfitters Registration will hold a Rulemaking Hearing on October 16, 2026, at 11:00 A.M. (MDT), regarding proposed revisions to Rule 1.5 – REGISTRATION MAINTENANCE AND REPORTING CHANGES, Rule 1.6 – RESPONSIBILITIES, PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT AND PROHIBITED CONDUCT, Rule 1.7 – SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS FOR OUTFITTING SERVICES, and Rule 1.10 – ADVISORY COMMITTEE. The purpose is to address the denied, revoked, or suspended licenses under 12-145-108(b)(5), C.R.S., acting as guides, under a registrant who cannot provide services when their license for hunting and/or fishing has been suspended or revoked by a wildlife Office; and, to reduce the Advisory Committee requirement from 5 to 3 members.
Statutory Authority 12-20-204, 12-145-107(1)(a) and 24-4-103, C.R.S
