Subjects and Issues Involved The proposed amendments to Chapter Two also implement changes from Senate Bill (SB) 25-192 (also referred to as the Sunset c Bill), which was signed into law on May 31, 2025. SB25-192 supported the expansion of the levels of state licensed or certified EMS providers who can provide out-of-hospital medical services for a Community Integrated Health Care Service (CIHCS) agency. This expansion was a priority item for stakeholders as Paramedics were the only level of EMS provider previously allowed to perform out-of-hospital medical services for a CIHCS agency. Now all categories of EMS providers may perform in-scope out-of-hospital medical services under CIHCS medical direction once they receive a Community Paramedicine endorsement. These proposed rules define and include the expanded out-of-hospital CIHCS scopes for all EMS providers with a Community Paramedicine endorsement (EMS-CPE) levels.
Statutory Authority Section 25-3.5-203(1)(a.5), C.R.S. and Section 25-3.5-206(4)(a), C.R.S. Other relevant statutes: Section 25-3.5-1301(II), C.R.S. (allows for out-of-hospital medical services to include care delivered by practitioners other than Community Paramedics)
