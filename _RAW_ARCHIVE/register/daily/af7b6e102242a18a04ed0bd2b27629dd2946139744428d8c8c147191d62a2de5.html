Subjects and Issues Involved These proposed rule amendments seek to add a methodology for identifying State-Designated Health Professional Shortage Areas (HPSA) specific to those insured by Colorado Medical Assistance Program (Medicaid). This rulemaking is authorized by Section 25-1.5-404 (1)(a) C.R.S. Promulgation of amended rules is requested because the PCO aims to better elucidate access to care needs specifically for Coloradans enrolled in Medicaid, which will support implementation of Colorado Health Service Corps (Section 25-1.5-501, et seq.) and related health systems planning for underserved communities. This rulemaking request is part of a planned expansion of the methodologies that intends to further describe the health professional workforce with respect to Medicaid-enrolled populations and Medicaid participating clinicians.
Statutory Authority Section 25-1.5-404 (1)(a) C.R.S.
