Subjects and Issues Involved This rule packet strikes language and processes that no longer align with the current definitions of or the use of Core Services at the county level. This rule packet provides clarification in sections where lack of clarity has historically caused confusion and inconsistency of Core use at the county level. Specifically, sections that speak to the length of time Core services can be provided, development of the yearly Core Plan, establishment of the Family Preservation Commission, and hard services available under Special Economic Assistance (SEA). This rule packet aligns language and processes in rule to streamline and simplify each county’s understanding and use of Core Services.
