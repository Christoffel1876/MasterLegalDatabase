Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Public Utilities Commission issues this Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (“NOPR”) to amend the Commission’s Rules Regulating Electric Utilities, 4 Code of Colorado Regulations (“CCR”) 723-3 (“Electric Rules”), to improve on the Commission’s transmission planning requirements as indicated by prior Commission decisions and to implement certain provisions of House Bill 26-1081. Specifically, through this NOPR we propose changes to Rules 3102, 3206, 3627 and the transmission-related rules incorporated into the Commission’s electric resource planning rules (Rules 3605, 3608, 3613, 3616). The Commission is also proposing two sets of additional rules. Proposed Rules 3890 – 3893 regard the assessment of cost effective opportunities for and plans for the implementation of Advanced Transmission Technologies on the existing transmission grid. Proposed Rules 3870-3872 are intended to exclude the collection of transmission expenditures deemed to be in the ordinary course of business through the Transmission Cost Adjustment (“TCA”) unless the Commission has explicitly found that such expenditures do not require a CPCN, and to generally limit cost recovery through the TCA to transmission facilities resulting in a net increase in capacity.
Statutory Authority The statutory authority for the proposed rules is found at: §§ 24-4-101 et seq.; §§ 40-2-108; 40-2-126, and 40-2-134, C.R.S.
