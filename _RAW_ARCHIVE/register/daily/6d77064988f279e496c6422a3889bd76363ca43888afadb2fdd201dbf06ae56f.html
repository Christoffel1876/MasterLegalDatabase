Rulemaking Hearing

Location 1707 Cole Blvd, Suite 300 Lakewood, CO 80401

Subjects and Issues Involved The State Licensing Authority is considering rule revisions to the Colorado Marijuana Rules at 1 CCR 212-3. The State Licensing Authority will consider: New or revised rules to clarify application submission requirements, regulated marijuana transfer restrictions and requirements, revisions to accelerator licensee eligibility requirements, expiration date and use-by date clarification. New or revised testing requirements to improve clarity, consistency, and compliance with required test types, testing and sampling procedures, and testing facility certification requirements. Revising and clarifying Regulated Marijuana Business Operator License privileges and restrictions. Application and license fee increases, consistent with section 44-10-801(1)(a), C.R.S. Establishing a new late fee that will apply when licensees fail to make the required second license fee payment. Establishing a new fee to cover the Division’s costs to process court appointments for Individual and Entity Temporary Appointee Registrations. Reducing the delivery permit fees to accurately reflect the direct and indirect costs of processing the permit application and enforcement of the delivery program. Final adopted rules may include any other new or revised rule necessary to implement the Marijuana Code. Please see the attached draft rule revisions for more detail regarding the subject of this rulemaking.
Statutory Authority The State Licensing Authority promulgates these rules pursuant to the authority granted in the Marijuana Code sections 44-10-101 et seq., C.R.S., Article XVIII, Section 16 of the Colorado Constitution, and the State Administrative Procedure Act at section 24-4-103, C.R.S.
