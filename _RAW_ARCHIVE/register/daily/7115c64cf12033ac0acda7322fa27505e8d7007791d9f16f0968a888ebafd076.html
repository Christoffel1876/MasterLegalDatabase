Subjects and Issues Involved Rule 8 RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE CLASS R DRIVER TESTING AND EDUCATION PROGRAM The Driver Testing and Education unit within the Department of Revenue, Division of Motor Vehicles, establishes the rules, regulations, and certification requirements for Certified Commercial Driving Schools (CCDS), Basic Operator Skills Testing Organizations (BOSTO), and Certified Employees. These rules furnish guidelines to ensure legal compliance, promote public safety and aid in the detection of fraudulent activities.
