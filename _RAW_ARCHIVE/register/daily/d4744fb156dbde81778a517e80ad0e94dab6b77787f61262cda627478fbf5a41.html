Subjects and Issues Involved The purpose of this regulation is to set forth requirements for Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs) regarding the verification that a pharmacy is a rural independent pharmacy, and establish corresponding annual reporting requirements for PBMs to demonstrate compliance with pharmacy reimbursement § 10-16-122.3(1.5) C.R.S. This regulation further outlines documentation requirements for inquiries and audits conducted by the Colorado Division of Insurance (Division).
Statutory Authority This regulation is being promulgated and adopted by the Commissioner of Insurance under the authority of §§ 10-1-109, 10-16-109, and 10-16-122.3(4), C.R.S.
