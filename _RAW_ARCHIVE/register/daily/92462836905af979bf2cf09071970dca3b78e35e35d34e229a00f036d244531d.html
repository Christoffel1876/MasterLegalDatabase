Subjects and Issues Involved The purpose of this rulemaking is to repeal these rules in accordance with HB 26-1395, which repealed statute § 24-33.5-1239, C.R.S. This statute created the Wildfire Resilient Homes Grant Program. With the repeal of this program, these rules are obsolete.
