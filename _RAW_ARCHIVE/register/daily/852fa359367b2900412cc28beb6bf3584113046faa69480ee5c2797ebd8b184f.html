Agency 213 - Natural Medicine Division
CCR Number 1 CCR 213-1
Rule Title Natural Medicine Division Rules

Rulemaking Hearing

Date 11/04/2026
Time 09:30 AM
Location 1707 Cole Blvd. Ste 300, Lakewood, CO 80401 and virtual

Subjects and Issues Involved The State Licensing Authority is considering rule revisions to the Colorado Natural Medicine Rules at 1 CCR 213-1. Specifically, the State Licensing Authority will consider: Implementing the provisions of HB 26-1325, including but not limited to: Establishing a new Limited Regulated Natural Medicine Sales License, including license eligibility, privileges, restrictions, and new and renewal application and license fees; Establishing a new Temporary Premises Permit, including permit eligibility, privileges, restrictions, and application and permit fees; Eliminating the restriction that an Owner may not have a Financial Interest in more than five natural medicine licenses; Implementing a required testing waiver process in the event there are no licensed Natural Medicine Testing Facilities to conduct required testing; Clarifying application requirements for Owner Entity licensees and Owner licensees; Amending required Administration Session log and reporting contents; And any other revisions necessary to implement the Regulated Natural Medicine Program.
Statutory Authority The State Licensing Authority promulgates these rules pursuant to the authority granted in the Natural Medicine Code sections 44-50-101 et seq., C.R.S., and the State Administrative Procedure Act at section 24-4-103, C.R.S.
