Subjects and Issues Involved This rule packet will update Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) regulations to allow for the Colorado Department of Human Services (CDHS) to approve county-initiated pilots that alter regulations or processes as a means of innovating the program by testing changes to the standard operating procedures or rules. This rule change is necessary as Colorado works toward reducing our Payment Error Rate (PER) through innovative solutions. Reducing PER is essential to avoid a catastrophic fiscal impact on the state. In order to do so effectively, we need flexibility (where legally allowable) to test out policy and process changes on a smaller scale to gather impact on errors, county workload impacts, and client experience prior to implementing state-wide. This means that any large policy or process change will be informed by evidence, thus reducing negative impacts to county staff or SNAP recipients.
