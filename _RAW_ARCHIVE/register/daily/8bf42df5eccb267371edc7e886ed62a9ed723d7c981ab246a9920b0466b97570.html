Notice of Rulemaking Hearing

Tracking Number 2026-00322
Department 1000 - Department of Public Health and Environment
Agency 1001 - Air Quality Control Commission
CCR Number 5 CCR 1001-33
Rule Title REGULATION NUMBER 29 EMISSION REDUCTION REQUIREMENTS FOR LAWN AND GARDEN EQUIPMENT

Rulemaking Hearing

Date 11/18/2026
Time 04:30 PM
Location Hybrid: Virtual via Zoom & 4300 Cherry Creek Drive South, Denver, CO 80246

Subjects and Issues Involved The Air Quality Control Commission (Commission) will hold a rulemaking hearing to consider revisions to Regulation Number 29 to revise reporting deadlines and adopt a summer time use-restriction on small and hand-held string trimmers, hedge trimmers, blowers, edgers, pruners, and push or walk-behind lawn mowers with an internal combustion engine of 7kW (10 horsepower) or smaller used by commercial operators in the ozone nonattainment area for which there are readily available electric alternatives. The proposed revisions are state-only revisions and may include clean-up, correction, and/or clarification revisions.
