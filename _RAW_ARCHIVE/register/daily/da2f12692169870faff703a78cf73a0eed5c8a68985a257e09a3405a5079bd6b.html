Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado State Board of Social Work Examiners will hold a Permanent Rulemaking Hearing on Friday, November 13, 2026, at 9:30 A.M. (MDT), regarding proposed revisions to Rule 1.14 – LICENSURE BY EXAMINATION (C.R.S. § 12-245-404). The purpose of the proposed revisions is to implement Colorado House Bill 26-1002 (CONCERNING MEASURE TO INCREASE PATIENT ACCESS TO BEHAVIORAL HEALTH PROVIDERS, AND, IN CONNECTION THEREWITH, EFFORTS TO ENHANCE PROVIDER PARTICIPATION IN HEALTH-CARE PROVIDER NETWORKS, REIMBURSEMENT OF PRELICENSED PROVIDERS WHO PROVIDE MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES UNDER THE SUPERVISION OF A LICENSED PROVIDER, AND DECREASING THE CLINICAL HOURS REQUIRED TO BECOME A LICENSED CLINICAL SOCIAL WORKER).
Statutory Authority 12-20-204, 12-245-204(4)(a), 12-245-222 and 24-4-103, C.R.S.
