Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Medical Board will hold a Permanent Rulemaking Hearing on Thursday, August 27, 2026, at 11:00 A.M. (MDT), to allow stakeholders a final opportunity to testify before the Board decides on proposed revisions: to create a pathway to full licensure for certain distinguished foreign teaching licensees in Rule 1.6; to repeal the substance use disorder continuing education requirements of Rule 1.29 as required by Colorado Senate Bill 26-138 (CONCERNING MEASURES TO REDUCE THE ADMINISTRATIVE BURDEN ON THE HEALTH-CARE SYSTEM); and to clarify which licensees are subject to the continuing education requirements set forth in Rule 1.33; and to implement Colorado House Bill 26-1044 (CONCERNING MEASURES TO IMPROVE EQUITY IN MATERNAL HEALTH).
Statutory Authority 12-20-204(1), 12-240-106(1)(a) and 24-4-103, C.R.S.
