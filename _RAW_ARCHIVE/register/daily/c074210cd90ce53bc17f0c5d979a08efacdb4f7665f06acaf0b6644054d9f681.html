Subjects and Issues Involved Pursuant to the passing of SB 26-131, The Division of Gaming is promulgating Rules regarding the prohibition of sports betting operators from accepting deposits using a credit card, either directly or indirectly, including through an account funded by a credit card, in connection with the acceptance of a sports bet. Specifically, Rules 7.6 Sport betting system requirements, 7.7 Sports betting kiosks, and 7.11 Sports betting accounts and the Basis and Purpose for Rule 7 are included. An Emergency rule was filed on 07/17/2026, to comply with the new law that will become effective on August 12, 2026.
