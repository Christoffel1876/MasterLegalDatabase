Subjects and Issues Involved The purpose of this rulemaking is several-fold. First, the rule amendment increases fees related to inspections and plan reviews under Article 14 of these rules in order to defray the anticipated costs of the program. Second, the rule amendment removes the provisions related to and for separate contractor and inspector categories to accommodate fire suppression systems installed in premanufactured buildings. Third, the rule amendment removes the provision requiring an Affidavit of Legal Residency under Section 3.4.5 of these rules and updates related language in Section 5.2.9. Fourth, the rule amendment updates the requirements for contractors registered or seeking registration by the Division to install and work on underground fire suppression system supply lines. Lastly, the rule amendment corrects and updates grammar and formatting, definitions, and Division contact information.
