Subjects and Issues Involved This rule packet will bring 12 CCR 2509-8 rules in Sections 7.701, 7.705 and 7.714 in compliance with HB 25-1172. The bill allows for a secure perimeter fence at the Psychiatric Residential Treatment Facility owned by the Colorado Department of Human Services and located on the grounds of the Fort Logan campus.
