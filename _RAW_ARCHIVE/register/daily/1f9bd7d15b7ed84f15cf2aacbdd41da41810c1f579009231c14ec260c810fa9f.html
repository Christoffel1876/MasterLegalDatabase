Subjects and Issues Involved By this Decision, the Colorado Public Utilities Commission (“Commission”) issues this Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (“NOPR”) to amend the Rules Regulating Telecommunications Services and Providers of Telecommunications Services contained in 4 Code of Colorado Regulations (“CCR”) 723-2-2890 through 2894 (collectively, “the No-Call List Rules”). The changes proposed are intended to reflect changes in Colorado statute enacted through the enactment of House Bill 26-1326 (“The PUC Sunset Bill” or “Sunset Bill”), which, among other things, increased the cap on the sliding scale of fees that the Commission must set annually to be paid by telemarketing firms, telemarketers, and other persons needing access to the Colorado No-Call List. Additionally, the legislation requires the Commission to establish, by rule, a separate fee charged to “conforming list brokers,” meaning “any person or entity that provides lists for the purpose of telephone solicitation, if such lists shall have removed, at a minimum of every thirty days, any phone numbers that are included on the Colorado no-call list.” § 6-1-903(4), C.R.S.
Statutory Authority The statutory authority for the rules proposed in this Proceeding is found at § 6-1-905, C.R.S.
