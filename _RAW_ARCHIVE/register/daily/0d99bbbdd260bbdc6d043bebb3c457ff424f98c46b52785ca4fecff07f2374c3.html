Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Office of Barber and Cosmetology Licensure will hold a Rulemaking Hearing on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at 2:00 P.M. (MDT), regarding proposed revisions to Rule 1.1 – ABBREVIATIONS AND DEFINITIONS, Rule 1.2 – LICENSURE BY EXAMINATION, Rule 1.3 – PROHIBITED EXAMINATION CONDUCT, Rule 1.4 – LICENSURE BY ENDORSEMENT, Rule 1.5 – LICENSE MAINTENANCE, REPORTING, AND REINSTATEMENT REQUIREMENTS, Rule 1.6 - REGISTRATION OF PLACES OF BUSINESS, Rule 1.7 – REQUIREMENTS FOR INFECTION CONTROL AND PREVENTION, Rule 1.8 – PROHIBITED ACTS AND SCOPE OF PRACTICE and Rule 1.9 – ADDITIONAL PRACTICES AND TRAINING REQUIREMENTS, Rule 1.10 – INSPECTIONS and Rule 1.11 – DECLARATORY ORDERS. The purpose of the proposed revisions is to implement Colorado House Bill 26-1181 (CONCERNING THE CONTINUATION OF THE “BARBER AND COSMETOLOGIST ACT”, AND, IN CONNECTION THEREWITH, IMPLEMENTING THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE DEPARTMENT OF REGULATORY AGENCIES IN THE DEPARTMENT'S 2025 SUNSET REPORT).
Statutory Authority 12-20-204, 12-105-106(1)(a) and 24-4-103, C.R.S
