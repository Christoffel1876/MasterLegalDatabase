Rule Title STANDARDS FOR COMMUNITY INTEGRATED HEALTH CARE SERVICE AGENCIES

Rulemaking Hearing

Date 08/19/2026
Time 10:00 AM
Location Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment, 4300 Cherry Creek Drive South, Denver, CO 80246

Subjects and Issues Involved In response to the statutory DORA Sunset review and report recommendations, SB25-192 which brought changes to statutes relevant to the rules, and changes to the Emergency Medical Service provider scope of practice brought by the Emergency Medical Practice Advisory Council (EMPAC), contained in 6 CCR 1015-3 necessitates this rule change to maintain alignment between the related rules with numerous additional topics for improvement identified. This rule also implements a 61% reduction in licensing fees for CIHCS agencies, correlating to fiscal amount adjustments for this program; this fee reduction is driven by an evaluation of funds spent on the work of licensing, and an analysis that a lower fee will not result in a reduction of oversight. The proposed changes will update and clarify the rules, support current industry standards, and streamline the organization and flow of the rule which lessens the cost and burden of implementation and maintenance on licensees. The improvements to the rule are broadly supported by stakeholders and incorporates their feedback.
Statutory Authority Section 25-3.5-1303, C.R.S. – Requiring establishing licensing requirements of Community Integrated Health Care Service Agencies Section 24-4-103.3, C.R.S. – Requiring periodic review and update of Department rules.
