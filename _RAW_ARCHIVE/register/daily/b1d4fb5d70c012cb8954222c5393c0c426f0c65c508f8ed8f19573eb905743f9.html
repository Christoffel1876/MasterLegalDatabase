Subjects and Issues Involved This rule change will update Colorado regulations to reflect the annual cost-of-living adjustments that the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA), Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA), make every year for the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) at the start of the federal fiscal year (FFY). The adjustments are for the core figures that go into determining eligibility and benefit amounts (income eligibility standards, benefit allotments, and deductions). Since SNAP is a federal program, we are required to administer the program as dictated by FNA. This rule change will ensure that Colorado regulations reflect the correct figures, ensuring compliance with federal law.
