Subjects and Issues Involved This will be a joint hearing on workers' compensation rules of procedure 16 and 18. Section 8-47-107, C.R.S. provides the Director of the Division of Workers’ Compensation with authority to adopt and amend proper rules and regulations to govern the proceedings and hearings of the Division. The Director also is given authority and directed to adopt rules concerning specific matters that fall within the Workers’ Compensation Act. Rule 16 sets forth the procedural standards related to medical services provided to the Colorado injured workers. These procedural standards, among other things, touch upon referrals, prior authorization, required billing forms and codes, as well as time frames for timely submission of medical bills and payments. The amendments this year revise the definitions of certain medical providers; clarify the prior authorization requirements and denials of prior authorizations using an Independent Medical Examination; and update the requirements related to denials of medical bills. Rule 18 (Fee Schedule) is required by statute. This rule sets out maximum allowable fees for medical care and related services falling within the purview of the Colorado Workers’ Compensation Act. The amendments revise, clarify, and update language, terminology, procedures, requirements, billing codes, fees, payments, conversion factors, and relative value units on which the fee schedule is based.
Statutory Authority Sections 8-47-107 and 8-42-101(3)(a)(I), C.R.S.
