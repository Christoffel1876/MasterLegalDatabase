Notice of Rulemaking Hearing

Tracking Number 2026-00323
Department 1000 - Department of Public Health and Environment
Agency 1001 - Air Quality Control Commission
CCR Number 5 CCR 1001-2
Rule Title COMMON PROVISIONS REGULATION

Rulemaking Hearing

Date 10/15/2026
Time 09:00 AM
Location Hybrid: Virtual via Zoom & 4300 Cherry Creek Drive South, Denver, CO 80246

Subjects and Issues Involved The Air Quality Control Commission will hold a written comment only rulemaking hearing to consider revisions to the Common Provisions Regulation to adjust the state-only maximum civil penalty based on the percentage change in the United States Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Price Index for Denver-Aurora-Lakewood from July 2025 to July 2026. The proposed revisions may include clean-up, correction, and/or clarification revisions.
Statutory Authority § 25-7-122, C.R.S.
