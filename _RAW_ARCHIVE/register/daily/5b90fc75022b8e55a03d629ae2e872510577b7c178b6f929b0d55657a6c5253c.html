Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Board of Accountancy will hold a Rulemaking Hearing on October 21, 2026 at 9:00 A.M. to allow stakeholders a final opportunity to testify before the Board decides on proposed revisions to Rule 1.4 – BOARD ORGANIZATION AND ADMINISTRATION, Rule 1.5 - EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS FOR EXAMINATION AND CERTIFICATION, Rule 1.6 - EXAMINATION GENERAL REQUIREMENTS AND PROHIBITED CONDUCT, Rule 1.7 - EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS FOR CERTIFICATION, Rule 1.8 - REQUIREMENTS FOR CERTIFICATION, Rule 1.9 - CERTIFICATE REQUIREMENTS, DISCIPLINE, MAINTENANCE, AND STATUS CHANGES and Rule 1.14 – PRACTICE PRIVILEGE/MOBILITY. The purpose is to implement Colorado Senate Bill 26-076 (CONCERNING THE PRACTICE IF CERTIFIED PUBLIC ACCOUNTANTS) and to correct deficiencies in the incorporation by reference provisions.
