Subjects and Issues Involved Commissioner Rules are programmatic-specific rules and is the mechanism for how BHA shares its vision of Colorado's behavioral health system. Content is to include matters of internal administration, correcting the language in the Volume from previous Office of Behavioral Health verbiage and structure to BHA's current structure, updating repealed regulatory language in the Volume, and aligning with statutory requirements to promulgate rules for the Veteran's Mental Health Services grant program.
Statutory Authority Section 27-50-104, C.R.S., provides the statutory authority for Behavioral Health Administration's Commissioner to adopt "Commissioner Rules". Section 28-5-714(5)(c), C.R.S., provides the statutory authority for Behavioral Health Administration's Commissioner to adopt Commissioner Rules for the Veteran's Mental Health Services program.
