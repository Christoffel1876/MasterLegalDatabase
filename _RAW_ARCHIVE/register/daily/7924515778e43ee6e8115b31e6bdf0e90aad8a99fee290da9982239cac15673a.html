Subjects and Issues Involved This rulemaking process has been initiated as a result of Senate Bill 25-200 regarding Dyslexia Screening and C.R.S. 22-7-1209(1)(b) regarding requirements to review the list of approved READ Act reading assessments every four years and update the list as necessary. These rule amendments are intended to include requirements for universal screening of K-3 students for characteristics of dyslexia. This includes: updates and revisions to definitions, updates to READ plan creation and parent involvement, implementation of universal dyslexia screening, updates to the minimum reading competency skill levels for grades K-3, and details for the department’s required review of assessments.
Statutory Authority These rules are promulgated pursuant to Colorado Revised Statutes sections 22-7-1208(6)(f), 1208(6.5)(d), 1208(6.7)(d), and 1209(1), which authorize the Colorado State Board of Education to promulgate Rules for the Administration of the Colorado Reading to Ensure Academic Development Act (the Colorado READ Act).
