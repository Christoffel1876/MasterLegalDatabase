Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Public Utilities Commission issues this Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (“NOPR”) to amend the Commission’s Rules Regulating Electric Utilities, 4 Code of Colorado Regulations (“CCR”) 723-3 (“Electric Rules”), and its Rules Regulating Gas Utilities, 4 CCR 723-4 (“Gas Rules”), to implement certain statutory provisions enacted by House Bill (“HB”) 23-1281. The proposed amendments to the Electric Rules and Gas Rules are intended to implement the following recently enacted statutory provision: § 40 2 138, C.R.S., regarding projects for the production of clean hydrogen, and specifically, § 40 2 138(3)(a)(II),C.R.S., requiring the Commission to promulgate rules to establish requirements for lifecycle greenhouse gas emission rate accounting for clean hydrogen projects.
Statutory Authority The statutory authority for the proposed rules is found generally at § 40-3-102(1),C.R.S. (authorizing the Commission to promulgate implementing rules) and § 40-2-108, C.R.S. (authorizing the Commission to promulgate rules necessary to administer and enforce Title 40). The statutory authority for these rules is also set forth specifically at § 40-2-138, C.R.S.
