Subjects and Issues Involved The purpose of this rulemaking is to consider amending or repealing administrative rules of the real estate broker program to ensure uniform and proper administration, implementation, and enforcement of Colorado laws and to reflect statutory changes resulting from recently enacted legislation: HB26-1287 and HB26-1426. Specifically, the Commission proposes repealing rule definitions that duplicate defined statutory term definitions and amend other rule definitions to clarify them. The Commission also proposes amending rules to clarify and update broker: license requirements; insurance and audit requirements; continuing education requirements and continuing education audits; separate accounts accounting requirements; professional standards; standard forms requirements; declaratory orders; and exceptions filings. Please be advised that the proposed amended rules and proposed rules for repeal are subject to further changes and modifications after public comment and the formal hearing.
