Subjects and Issues Involved On July 4, 2025, House Resolution 1 (H.R.1) was signed into law. This federal law contained several provisions that changed eligibility rules for the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP). Under these provisions, eligibility criteria have been revised to include modifications to how energy assistance payments are treated in the SNAP budget, restrictions on the non-citizen statuses that are eligible for SNAP, and an expansion of SNAP work requirements. This rule change will bring Colorado SNAP regulations into alignment with the changes made to the program by H.R.1. Additionally, Colorado rule has neglected to include a provision surrounding home address changes reported mid-certification and how that impacts shelter costs. This has been added to this rule package to ensure that Colorado regulations are compliant with existing federal regulations regarding this matter.
