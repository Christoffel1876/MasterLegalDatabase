Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Public Utilities Commission issues this Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (“NOPR”) to amend the Commission’s Rules Regulating Electric Utilities, 4 Code of Colorado Regulations (“CCR”) 723-3, to implement certain provisions of House Bill (“HB”) 26 1007 related to meter collar adapters. The 2026 Colorado General Assembly recently enacted through HB 26-1007 provisions related to meter collar adapters with the aim to reduce or avoid costly and time consuming service panel upgrades when electric utility customers interconnect distributed energy resources (“DERs”). The new statutory provisions, codified primarily at § 40-2-124(1.3), C.R.S., apply to investor-owned electric utilities as well as cooperative electric associations, both of which are statutorily defined as qualifying retail utilities subject to the interconnection rules adopted by the Commission. Municipally owned electric utilities are further required to adopt interconnection standards that are functionally similar to those established by the Commission. However, § 40-2-124(7)(c), C.R.S., codifies the specific requirements that apply to municipally owned electric utilities.
Statutory Authority The statutory authority for the proposed rules is found at: §§ 24-4-101 et seq. and §§ 40-2-108 and 40-2-124, C.R.S.
