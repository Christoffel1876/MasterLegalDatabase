Subjects and Issues Involved This rulemaking modifies three different topics. The first topic relates to statutory requirements in section 25-1.5-103, (1)(a)(I)(C), C.R.S, related to extended licensing survey cycles and tiered inspections. This language update is in response to recommendations from the Health Facility Licensing Program and Cash Funds Performance Audit published in July 2025. The second topic aligns rule with a change to the statutory definition of restraint in Section 26-20-101, C.R.S, brought by HB25-1248. The third topic aligns rule with various statutory changes in Sections 12-280-135, and 12-280-135.5, C.R.S, regarding medicine donation programs, brought by SB25-289.
Statutory Authority Section 25-1.5-103, C.R.S.
