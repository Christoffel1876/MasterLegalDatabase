Subjects and Issues Involved The Division is requesting amendments to align and conform Chapter One to another rule set in 6 CCR 1015-3 that are required to maintain consistency with the proposed Chapter Two rule revision and their originating law. The Chapter Two amendments primarily impact Chapter One due to its expansion of the EMS provider levels that are now eligible to obtain a Community Paramedicine Endorsement implemented from SB25-192. All levels of EMS providers (EMT, AEMT, EMT-I and Paramedics) will be authorized to perform in-scope CIHCS services under CIHCS medical direction once the endorsement is received. The Chapter Two rules define and include the expanded out-of-hospital CIHCS scopes for all EMS Providers with a Community Paramedicine Endorsement (EMS-CPE), requiring the Chapter One rules to also include these new levels of endorsed providers in its relevant education, certification and licensure rules. The proposed Chapter One rules include all relevant new definitions, as well as the addition of these newly endorsed providers in the initial and renewal certification provisions; as well as a limited number of other amendments that either conform to other Chapter Two proposed rules or clarifying existing Chapter One rules. Without further discussion, President Butts closed the request.
Statutory Authority Section 25-3.5-101, et seq., C.R.S.
