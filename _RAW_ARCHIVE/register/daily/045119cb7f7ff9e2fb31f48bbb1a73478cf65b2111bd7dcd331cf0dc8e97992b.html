Subjects and Issues Involved with Revised Uniform Unclaimed Property Act (RUUPA) statute changes enacted June 4, 2025. Changes include: updating record retention requirements from ten(10) years to six (6) years, removing the Local Government Agency Opt-Out Option, increasing the notary requirement from $1,000 to $2,500, adding sections on Negative Reporting, Subpoenas, and Interest & Penalties, as well as formatting and grammatical updates.
Statutory Authority The Revised Uniform Unclaimed Property Act (RUUPA), codified at CRS 38-13-101 et. seq., provides the legislative authority.
