### Challenges list (continuation)

- New fee categories do not align with the County’s existing accounting structures and **Energov system**.
- Duplicative or overlapping categories causing confusion.
- Some fee amounts very similar (e.g., **$5 to $10 difference**) for similar services, adding complexity.
- The “**equity fee reduction**” option has been beneficial but the current resolution does not account for all beneficial situations.

### Recommended Changes to Fee Schedule (bold + underlined)

- Full (100%) fee amounts scheduled **January 2, 2024**; staff recommend minor adjustments for customer experience:
  1. Combine similar fee categories; use averaging or cost recovery data for slight differences.
  2. Round fees to the nearest **$5 increment**.
  3. Eliminate duplicative or unnecessary fees.
  4. Add the **Consumer Price Index (CPI)** factor when building permit fees are adjusted in **July 2024** (instead of January).
  5. Reorganize the fee structure for ease of use.
- At **November 20 work session**, staff proposed absorbing meeting publication and notification letter costs into fee categories; supported by Commissioners but needs more analysis; reconsider later.

### Recommended Changes to Equity Fee Resolution (bold + underlined)

- Resolution establishes lower fees for small-scale projects and allows Director discretion for projects fitting the **Comprehensive Plan**; set to expire **December 31, 2023**.

### Equity eligibility (continuation)

- Small businesses registered in Larimer County with three (3) or fewer employees doing site plan or tenant finish;
- Subdivisions with fewer than three (3) lots;
- Minor modifications or site upgrades to achieve Comprehensive Plan or Climate and Sustainability Plan goals (may include but not limited to dark sky lighting, water efficiency landscaping, renewable energy infrastructure on a commercial site, or local food related agricultural projects); and
- At the discretion of the Director or by the Director in consultation with the Board of County Commissioners, other projects that may achieve Comprehensive Plan, Climate and Sustainability Plan goals, or other Larimer County Strategic Plan objectives.

**Staff recommends extending the Equity Fee Reduction Resolution for 4 more years (to 2027), with the following adjustments:**

- Small business category: broaden max employees from 3 to 10 and qualifying application types to any application type
- Add category for affordable housing projects
- Add category for accessory uses on agricultural properties
- Remove equity fee option for Extended Family Dwellings (already subject to a lower fee than similar project types)
