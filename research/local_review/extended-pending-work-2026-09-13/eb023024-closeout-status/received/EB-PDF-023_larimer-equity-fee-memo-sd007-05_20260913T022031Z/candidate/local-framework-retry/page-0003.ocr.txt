Staff recommends extending the Equity Fee Reduction Resolution for 4 more years (to 2027), with
the following adjustments:
• For the small business category, broaden the language to increase the maximum number of
employees (from 3 to 10) and the types of development applications that qualify (any
application type)
• Add a category for affordable housing projects
• Add a category for accessory uses on agricultural properties
• Remove the equity fee option for Extended Family Dwellings, which are already subject to a
lower fee than similar project types
