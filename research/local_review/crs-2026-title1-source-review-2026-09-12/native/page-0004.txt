PART 1 
DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS 
1-1-101.    Short title.   
Articles 1 to 13 of this title shall be known and may be cited as the "Uniform Election Code of 
1992"; within these articles, "this code" means the "Uniform Election Code of 1992". 
 
Source: L. 92: Entire article R&RE, p. 624, § 1, effective January 1, 1993. 
 
Editor's note: This section is similar to former § 1-1-101 as it existed prior to 1992.
