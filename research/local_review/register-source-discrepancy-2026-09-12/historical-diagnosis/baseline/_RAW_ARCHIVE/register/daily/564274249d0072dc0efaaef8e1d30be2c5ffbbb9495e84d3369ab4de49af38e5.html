Subjects and Issues Involved The Colorado Public Utilities Commission (“Commission”) issues this Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (“NOPR”) to consider amendments to the Commission’s State Safety Oversight (“SSO”) rules, which currently appear as a subset of the Rules Regulating Railroads, Rail Fixed Guideways, Transportation by Rail, and Rail Crossing (“Rail Rules”), 4 Code of Colorado Regulations (“CCR”) 723-7, at Rule 7340–7363, 4 CCR 723-7. The primary purpose of this rulemaking is to relocate the SSO rules from the general Rail Rules (4 CCR 723-7) into a new, standalone Part: 4 CCR 723-14. This shift is intended to improve the identification, accessibility, and readability of the SSO rules. Additionally, the Commission proposes updates to better align the SSO rules with the Federal Transit Administration’s regulations, specifically, 49 C.F.R. Part 671 (minimum safety standards for Rail Transit Roadway Worker programs) and 49 C.F.R. Part 674 (State Safety Oversight Program requirements for rail fixed guideway public transportation systems), implementing 49 U.S.C. § 5329. Finally, the Commission proposes targeted enhancements to the SSO rules to better ensure the safety of the traveling public in Colorado.
