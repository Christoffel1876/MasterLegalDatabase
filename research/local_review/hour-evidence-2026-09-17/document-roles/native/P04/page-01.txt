Administrative Revision Date: Not applicable 
Policy Custodian: Building Safety  
Purpose: Pursuant to the authority granted by C.R.S. §30-28-204, amending Jefferson 
County’s building codes for the purpose of prescribing regulations governing 
construction in unincorporated Jefferson County. 
A. Adoption 
1. The Board of County Commissioners amends Jefferson County’s building codes 
by adopting the following building codes (collectively, the “Jefferson County 
Building Codes”), effective June 30, 2026: 
a. The 2024 edition of the International Building Code, as modified by the 2024 
Jefferson County Commercial Building Code Supplement; 
b. The 2024 edition of the International Fuel Gas Code, as modified by the 2024 
Jefferson County Fuel Gas Code Supplement , and any modifications to or 
newer versions of the Colorado Fuel Gas Code that are adopted by the 
Colorado State Plumbing Board pursuant to its authority under C.R.S. §12-
155-106(2) which impose a higher standard shall be automatically 
incorporated herein and deemed to modify this code and shall become 
effective July 1 of the year following state adoption of such; 
c. The 2024 edition of the International Plumbing Code, as modified by the 2024 
Jefferson County Plumbing Code Supplement, and any modifications to or 
newer versions of  the Colorado Plumbing Code that are adopted by the 
Colorado State Plumbing Board pursuant to its authority under C.R.S. §12-
155-106(2) which impose a higher standard shall be automatically 
incorporated herein and deemed to modify this code and shall become 
effective July 1 of the year following state adoption of such;
