3. This Policy and the Jefferson County Building Codes adopted herein shall take 
effect June 30, 2026 and shall supersede and replace the existing building code 
Policy and all previously-adopted versions of such codes and building code 
supplements as of such date.  
4. The Jefferson County Building Codes shall apply to all unincorporated areas of 
Jefferson County. 
B. Severability 
Should any provision of this Policy or the Jefferson County Building Codes be 
adjudged by any court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid for any reason, such 
judgment shall not affect, impair or invalidate this Policy or the Jefferson County 
Building Codes as a whole or any part thereof other than the part declared to be 
invalid.
