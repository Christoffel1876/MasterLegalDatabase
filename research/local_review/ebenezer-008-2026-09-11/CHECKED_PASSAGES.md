## CC-ORD7A-TITLE-RECITALS-REPEAL (physical page 1)

ORDINANCE NO. 7-A
AN ORDINANCE TO REPEAL ORDINANCE NO. 7

WHEREAS, C.R.S. §30-28-111 et seq., authorizes boards of county commissioners to plan for and regulate the use of land; and

WHEREAS, C.R.S. §30-28-111 et seq., authorizes boards of county commissioners to adopt and amend zoning regulations pursuant to specified procedures for the purposes of promoting health, safety, morals, convenience, order, prosperity or welfare of the present and future inhabitants of the county; and

WHEREAS, C.R.S. §30-28-116 authorizes boards of county commissioners to amend county zoning regulations pursuant to specified procedures; and

WHEREAS, the County has heretofore enacted Ordinance No. 7 which purports to adopt the C.R.S.§30-28-124 and C.R.S. §30-28-124.5 dealing with penalties as they relate to zoning violations and establishes policies and procedures for zoning enforcement; and,

WHEREAS, the County has the authority to enforce the provisions of C.R.S. §30-28-124 and C.R.S. §30-28-124.5 without its adoption via Ordinance making Ordinance No. 7 cumulative and unnecessary; and

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners believes that it is in the best interest of the Citizens of the County to repeal Ordinance No. 7.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that Ordinance No. 7 is hereby repealed in full.

The document states repeal of Ordinance No. 7 and its rationale. It contains no explicit effective-date clause in these two pages; no presently applicable enforcement rule is inferred.
