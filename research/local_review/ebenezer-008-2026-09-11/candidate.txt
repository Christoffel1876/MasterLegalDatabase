ORDINANCE NO. 7-A
AN ORDINANCE TO REPEAL ORDINANCE NO. T
WHEREAS, C.R.S. §30-28-111 et seq., authorizes boards of county commissioners to plan for
and regulate the use of land; and
WHEREAS, C.R.S. §30-28-111 et seq., authorizes boards of county commissioners to adopt and
amend zoning regulations pursuant to specified procedures for the purposes of promoting health,
safety, morals, convenience, order, prosperity or welfare of the present and future inhabitants of
the county; and
WHEREAS, C.R.S. §30-28-116 authorizes boards of county commissioners to amend county
zoning regulations pursuant to specified procedures; and
WHEREAS, the County has heretofore enacted Ordinance No. 7 which purports to adopt the
C.R.S.§30-28-124 and C.R.S. §30-28-124.5 dealing with penalties as they relate to zoning
violations and establishes policies and procedures for zoning enforcement; and,
WHEREAS, the County has the authority to enforce the provisions of C.R.S. §30-28-124 and
C.R.S. §30-28-124.5 without its adoption via Ordinance making Ordinance No. 7 cumulative and
unnecessary; and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners believes that it is in the best interest of the
Citizens of the County to repeal Ordinance No. 7.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that Ordinance No. 7 is hereby repealed in full.
