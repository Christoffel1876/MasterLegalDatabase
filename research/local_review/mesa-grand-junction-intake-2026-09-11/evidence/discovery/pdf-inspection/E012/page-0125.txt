Exceptions 
a. 
 Zoning district height limits do not apply to belfries, cupolas, spires, domes, monuments, airway

the roof. 
SECTION 7.02 | AFT DISTRICT DENSITY  
A. 
Purpose  
In order to provide more certainty regarding appropriate levels of development on land within the Rural 
Planning Area, the County has adopted the provisions of this section to govern density in the AFT Zoning 
District .  
B. 
Density 
The base density allowed on lots and parcels zoned AFT shall be no more than an average of one lot per 
five (5) acres unless a different base density is identified in Table 7-1 (see below). If density bonus is allowed, 
the maximum density may only be achieved when an application for density bonus is submitted and 
approved subject to the criteria listed in Section 7.02 C.
