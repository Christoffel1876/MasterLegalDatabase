MESA COUNTY BUILDING DEPARTMENT FEE SCHEDULE 
1A
All fees related to the activities of the Mesa County Building Department in relation to public compliance 
with the adopted Building Codes are given in this Exhibit A. In general, fees include permit fees, plan 
review fees and inspection fees. All questions regarding the calculation of fees for any specific project 
should be directed to the Mesa County Building Department.
