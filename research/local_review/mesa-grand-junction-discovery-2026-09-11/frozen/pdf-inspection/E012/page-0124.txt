Lot Coverage  
Lot coverage is calculated by dividing the total square footage of all building footprints and all impervious 
surfaces by the square footage of the lot.

G. 
Height  
1. 
 Building height is measured as the vertical distance between the average finished grade at the base 
of the building along the side of the building being measured and:  
a. 
 The average height between the eaves and ridge line of a gable, hip or gambrel roof;  
b. 
The highest point of a mansard roof; or  
c. 
The highest point of the coping of a flat roof.
