3. 
Applicability 
a. 
The Overlay District provides for flexibility in the land use pattern within the Rural Community of 
Gateway by providing property owners with the option of developing in accordance with the 
underlying zoning or with the Overlay District standards of this section. 
b. 
It is the intent of the Overlay District to allow reasonable use of property consistent with the goals 
and policies of the “Gateway Rural Community Plan”. 
c. 
The Overlay District will provide property owners with a tool to address existing nonconforming 
uses and structures under current zoning. 
d. 
In addition to the Code standards for all developments in either Area “A” or Area “B” district, said 
developments are subject to the mandatory standards and design guidelines in Appendix E of 
this LDC if a development proposes using the optional overlay district.
