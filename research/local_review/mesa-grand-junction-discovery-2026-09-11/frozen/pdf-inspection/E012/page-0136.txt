3. 
Paving and Striping 
a. 
All required off-street parking areas within the Urban Zoning Districts shall be striped and paved 
with concrete, asphalt, pavers, or other material approved by the Director. Within Rural 
Communities, parking areas with more than five (5) spaces shall be paved and striped.  
b. 
In the Rural Zoning Districts, dust suppression shall be required for all non-paved off-street parking 
areas located within the Grand Valley Airshed.  
4. 
Circulation 
a. 
All required off-street parking areas shall be designed to provide safe, efficient circulation for 
vehicles and pedestrians. 
b. 
When an individual parking area is adjacent to another individual parking area, the design shall 
accommodate a cross-connection between them if possible.
