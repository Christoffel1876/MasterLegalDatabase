C. 
Concentrated Business District (B-2) 
The Concentrated Business District is primarily intended to accommodate concentrated retail, service, and 
office uses in community downtown settings. The district is not intended for major shopping centers or large 
outdoor sales areas. Pedestrian circulation is encouraged within the B-2 District through the use of flexible 
parking requirements and design standards.
