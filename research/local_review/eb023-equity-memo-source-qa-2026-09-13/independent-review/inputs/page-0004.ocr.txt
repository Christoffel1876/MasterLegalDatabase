Sheriff's Office Wildfire Review Fee
On December 11, 2023, the Board provided direction on new wildfire review fees for development
projects within the jurisdiction of the Larimer County Sheriff's Office. The Planning and Engineering
Development Review Fee Schedule has been updated to include those fees as proposed.
Next Steps
If adopted, staff will communicate the fee adjustments to customers and implement the new fee
schedule beginning January 2, 2024.
Staff recommends a follow-up review of the development review fee schedule every five vears to re-
evaluate the County's service cost recovery goal and make necessary adjustments. Accordingly, the
next major fee study would occur in 2027.
