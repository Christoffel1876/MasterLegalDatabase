(9)
Issue Administrative Permits
The PCD Director may review and issue administrative permits pursuant to 
the requirements and procedures of this Code.
(10)
Sign Plats and Other Administrative Documents
The PCD Director may sign plats and other documents relating to the 
application of this Code to specific property or development applications.
(11)
Interpret Maps and Regulatory Documents
The PCD Director may interpret maps and regulatory documents for their 
applicability to a development application, and consult with any other 
agencies in that interpretation. 
(12)
Delegate Decision Making Authority
The PCD Director may delegate decision making authority to staff on 
development applications, maps, or other matters in accordance with the 
decision-making procedures of the LDC.
