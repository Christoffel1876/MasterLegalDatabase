This action included authorization to annually adjust the Development Review Fee Schedule based on the same CPI index that is applied to the building permit fees.

Small Project Fee Reduction Information

Larimer County offers a 25% reduction in Planning and Engineering Development Review fees for certain application types that provide community benefits. Application types that are eligible for reduced fees can be found in the revised resolution below. Applicants must request the fee reduction at the time of application. This program is in effect through December 31, 2027.

  • BCC Equity Fee Reduction Memo for December 19, 2023

Application Withdrawal and Refund Policy

Larimer County Community Development is committed to providing a predictable and transparent development review process. Fees are collected at the time of application to cover the professional staff time and technical resources required to intake, process, and review an application. This policy applies only to development review fees paid directly to the County.

We understand that project plans may change, and applicants may occasionally need to withdraw an application. In these instances, a refund may be available based on the progress of the application at the time a formal written request for withdrawal is received.

Project Milestone ReachedRefund AmountNon-Refundable Portion
Intake & Completeness Review 80% of Application Fee20% or $150 (whichever is greater)
Initial Staff Review50% of Application Fee50% of Application Fee
Referral Stage & Beyond0% (No Refund)100% of Application Fee

The Referral Stage is defined as the date at which the application is formally distributed to internal and external agencies for review. Once an application reaches this stage or progresses further, significant County resources and coordination efforts have already been committed, and the fee is considered fully earned.

Non-Refundable Processing Costs

The non-refundable portion of the application fee covers the immediate cost of professional staff time and technical resources dedicated to a project upon submittal. Since these services are rendered immediately upon receipt of an application, this portion of the fee is retained to ensure we can maintain an efficient and equitable review system for all applicants.

How to Request a Withdrawal: To withdraw an application and request a refund, please submit a written request to PlanningCIRT@larimer.org.
