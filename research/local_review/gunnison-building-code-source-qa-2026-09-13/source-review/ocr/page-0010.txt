ATTACHMENT C
AMENDMENTS TO THE INTERNATIONAL MECHANICAL CODE, 2021 EDITION
Section 101.1 Title: Replace [Name of Jurisdiction] with "Gunnison County".
Section 109.2 Schedule of permit fees: Add the following:
The permit fees shall be established by multiplying the project valuation by 0.0075 and
the minimum permit fee shall be $55.00. The plan review fee, where plan review is
needed, shall be 65 percent (65%) of the permit fee.
Section 113 Means of Appeals, Section 113.1 General: Replace with the following:
The Gunnison County Board of Adjustment shallhear and decide appeals of orders,
decisions or determinations made by the code official relative to the application and
interpretation of this code.
Section 114.1 Board of Appeals: Replace with the following:
The Gunnison County Board of Appeals pursuant to C.R.S. § 30-28-118 shall be the
Gunnison County Board of Adjustment as described in the Gunnison County Land Use
Resolution Section 8-103: Appeals.
