# Delta resolution — source-first role observations

Atlas directly inspected all three complete 144-dpi page images before opening native text. The queue/filename and prior three-page metadata were known; this is not a blind review. This is a documentary-role review, not a full transcription or a legal-currentness determination.

1. Page 1 identifies the Board of County Commissioners of the County of Delta, State of Colorado. The resolution number is printed `2021-R-` followed by handwritten-looking `001`. Its visible title says it repeals Delta County land-use and subdivision regulations and adopts a new land-use code, including zoning maps.
2. The first recital describes land-use regulation in the unincorporated territory of the county. Other recitals mention a 2018 master plan; public work beginning in April 2019; a July 2020 draft; Planning Commission certification on October 14, 2020; notice on November 18, 2020; and a December 3, 2020 public meeting. These are distinct source assertions, not adoption dates.
3. Page 2 begins an operative `NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED` clause approving/adopting the land-use code as attached, including zoning maps. Paragraph 1 names the resolution, attached code and maps together as the Delta County Land Use Code.
4. Paragraph 2 names several prior county planning districts/protection plans in a repeal clause. Those names do not establish separate government owners. Paragraph 3 states that the code goes into force on April 1, 2021. It also describes a period in which no new applications for listed land-use actions will be accepted. This report does not assess that provision's present applicability.
5. Paragraph 4 describes repeal of older regulations with exceptions. Subparagraph (a) distinguishes applications submitted before January 5, 2021 and certain final-plat applications within twelve months of the resolution's effective date. Subparagraph (b) describes expiration of prior approvals and an extension route. No attempt is made here to resolve the legal meaning or effective-date referent.
6. Paragraphs 5–6 establish a three-member Board of Adjustment and contain severability language. Paragraph 6 continues from page 2 to page 3. Paragraphs 7–8 address public health/safety/welfare and inconsistent prior measures; paragraph 8 expressly excludes repeal of repealer clauses and revival of resolutions. Preserve the negative/revival language in later extraction.
7. Page 3 explicitly says `ADOPTED this 5th day of January, 2021.` This is separate from the April 1, 2021 code-effective statement. The page contains three signature marks above/over printed commissioner names/titles, a clerk/recorder attestation signature mark, and a visible county seal. Printed names include Mark Roeber, Don Suppes, Mike Lane and Teri Stephenson. Signature presence is observable; identities, authenticity and authority are not certified here.
8. All three pages bear `Page n of 3` footers. The complete three-page PDF contains the resolution, including its signature/attestation page. It does not reproduce the referenced land-use code or zoning maps. The separately retained 234-page 2024-labeled file has not been opened for this review; its exact relationship to this 2021 adopting instrument remains a separate question.
