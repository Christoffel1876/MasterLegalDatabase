## Table boundaries that must survive extraction

The third Administrative Amendment description is:

> Use by Special Review, Location and Extent, Final Development Plan,
> Preliminary Development Plan, Planned Sign Program, Master Development Plan

It belongs to one `$1,500` per-case row. The Minor amendment row immediately
below says `Minor adjustments to a plan.` and is `$500` per case.

Wireless Communication Facilities is `$1,500` per case near the top of the
administrative section. Its EFR row is a separate `$645` per-case entry at the
bottom of that section, after Extension and immediately before Major Cases.
Do not apply the Major Cases setup-fee header backward to those administrative
rows.

There is no standalone Preliminary Plat row on page 1. `Preliminary Development
Plan` does appear in the grouped Administrative Amendment description and the
last grouped Major Cases description on page 1. These are different labels.

On page 2, Rural Cluster is followed by indented Site Analysis and Conservation
Area Management Plan entries with blank left cells. Preserve that visible
layout and each fee association; do not fill the blank cells with an inferred
parent label or legal hierarchy.

Retain section payment instructions, the Major Cases `$500.00` setup-fee rule
and its `except where noted` qualification, row-specific setup-fee exceptions,
per-sheet/per-case/per-location units, engineering-fee referral, and the warning
that other agencies may bill separately. Rates alone are not a complete fee
statement, and this audit calculates no final application bill.
