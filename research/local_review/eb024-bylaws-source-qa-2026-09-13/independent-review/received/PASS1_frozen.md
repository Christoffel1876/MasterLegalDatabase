Header: CHAPTER 1 / BYLAWS / EL PASO COUNTY BOARD OF HEALTH

### SECTION 1.1: ORGANIZATION
- **A. Board of Health:** Public Health governed by Board of Health of El Paso County (“the Board”); nine members appointed by BOCC per Article 1 of Title 25, C.R.S.; no remuneration except actual expense reimbursement on official Public Health business.
- **B. Place of Business:** Principal office at El Paso County Public Health building, 1675 W. Garden of the Gods Road, Suite 1044, Colorado Springs, Colorado.
- **C. Authority:** (1) powers/duties Part 5 of Article 1 of Title 25, C.R.S.; (2) select/appoint Executive Director (public health, public administration or closely related); at will of Board; (3) determine/devise/set general policies for Executive Director and Public Health.
- **D. Mission:** promote and protect public health and environmental quality through people, prevention and partnerships, subject to funding; work toward 10 essential services (NALBOH).

### SECTION 1.2: OFFICERS
Regular officers: President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer. President/VP term not to exceed one (1) calendar year; election at regular December meeting or as Board determines.
- **A. President:** preside; interest in Public Health affairs; ex-officio member of all committees without vote; authority to sign deeds/instruments unless delegated / CRS.
- **B. Vice President:** perform President duties in absence, resignation, death, or disability.
