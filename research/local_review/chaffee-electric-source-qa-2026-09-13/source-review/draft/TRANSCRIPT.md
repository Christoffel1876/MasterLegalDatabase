CHAFFEE COUNTY COLORADO
ORDINANCE NO. 2026-01

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 2021 INTERNATIONAL ENERGY CONSERVATION CODE

WHEREAS, the Chaffee County Board of County Commissioners recognizes that adopting supporting amendments to the 2021 International Energy Conservation Code (“IECC”) will improve the construction and maintenance of the built environment and will thereby promote the health, safety, resiliency, affordability, sustainability, and general welfare of our community; and

WHEREAS, buildings are responsible for 40 percent of greenhouse gas emissions in the United States, and reducing building greenhouse emissions is essential for reducing the impacts of climate change, including mitigating local disaster events such as wildfires, droughts, and other severe weather; and

WHEREAS, Chaffee County desires to collaborate with other Colorado communities to adopt a consistent version of the IECC amendments;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF CHAFFEE COUNTY the following:

Section 1. The Board of Chaffee County Commissioners hereby makes and adopts the determinations and findings contained in the recitals set forth above.

Section 2. Chaffee County Colorado Ordinance 2023-02 is hereby amended to include the following.

<u>R404.4 Additional electric infrastructure. All combustion equipment shall be installed in accordance with Section R403.5.4 and shall be provided with a junction box that is connected to an electrical panel by continuous raceways and or conductors that meet the following requirements:</u>

1. <u>The junction box, raceway, and bus bar in the electric panel and conductors serving the electric panel and future equipment shall be sized to accommodate electric equipment sized to serve the same load as the combustion equipment.</u>
