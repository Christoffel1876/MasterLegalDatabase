Irrigation Permit Fee (required for all non-residential landscape and irrigation installations):  $604 
The Irrigation Permit Fee covers the following required initial activities / inspections: 
1.
Pre-construction meeting, Irrigation point of connection inspection, Soil inspection, Sub-Surface Drip (SDI) grid
inspection if applicable), Final landscape inspection / site compliance with approved plans, Final irrigation
inspection / site compliance with approved plans, Closeout of Irrigation Permit
2.
Re-inspection Fee: $108
3.
If any of the above items are not successfully completed at the time of initial inspection, or if punch list items are
generated during the final landscape and/or irrigation inspection, a standard re-inspection fee will be assessed.
This fee must be paid prior to the completion of any follow-up inspections or close-out of the permit
