Page 58 
 
 
 
 
Barricades 
1. 
Whenever roadways terminate due to project phasing, subdivision 
boundaries, etc., barricades shall be installed and maintained. 
2. 
Design and installation shall comply with the requirements of the MUTCD, 
most recent edition. 
3. 
The barricades shall be shown on the construction drawings.
