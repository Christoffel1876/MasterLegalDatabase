 SPECIFICATIONS 
Traffic signal specifications shall be developed in accordance with Town and 
CDOT’s Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Construction.  The 
designer will determine the need for project-specific construction special 
provisions.  Notes may be added to the construction plans if the designer feels 
that it is necessary to clarify certain items.
