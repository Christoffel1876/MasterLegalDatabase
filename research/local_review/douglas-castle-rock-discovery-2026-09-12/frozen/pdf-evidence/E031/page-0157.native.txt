 Subsurface Utility Engineering (SUE)  
 
1. 
A project requires a SUE if it meets all of the following conditions:  
 Involves a construction contract with a public entity, construction in 
the public ROW or easement, infrastructure that will be dedicated to 
the Town, or other work as determined by the Town Manager or 
designee; and 
 Primarily involves horizontal construction and does not primarily 
involve the construction of buildings; and 
 Anticipated excavation footprint exceeds two feet in depth and is at 
least a contiguous 1,000 square feet (excluding fencing and signing 
projects) or involves utility boring or any utility crossing of public 
water, sanitary or stormwater systems; and 
 Requires the design services of a licensed professional engineer.  
 
2. 
If all the above criteria are met, subsurface utility engineering 
documentation shall be required for approval of any Construction 
Documents and Right-of-Way Permits.  Note:  The Quality Levels A and B
