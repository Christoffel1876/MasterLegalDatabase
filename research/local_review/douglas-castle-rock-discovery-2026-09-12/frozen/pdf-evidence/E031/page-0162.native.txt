SECTION 14 – PAVEMENT DESIGN CRITERIA    -    April 2023 
Page 149 
 
 
 GENERAL 
All applicants must refer to the most recent version of the Metropolitan 
Government Pavement Engineers Council (MGPEC) “Pavement Design 
Standards” for pavement design requirements.  
 
 
 
Pavement Life Cycle Costs 
1. 
MGPEC’s Life Cycle Cost Analysis with Recommended Maintenance criteria 
shall include analysis of various pavement sections and the different types 
of maintenance treatments and schedules to assess the total lifecycle cost 
of each option.  Maintenance treatments assessed should use contracted 
market rates.  Lowest lifecycle costs should be calculated per the methods 
identified within the MGPEG standards.   
 
2. 
The considered pavement sections shall include:  
 The identified maintenance schedule with recommended treatments at 
year points from construction/reconstruction 
 Unit cost ($/lane-mile) for the initial construction, or reconstruction, 
and each maintenance treatment 
 Total lifecycle unit cost ($/lane-mile/year).   
 Total lifecycle unit cost should be reported for other sections and 
schedules not selected. 
 
 
Alleys 
Public and private alleys require pavement design approved by the Town of 
Castle Rock.
