 Corner Clearance 
It is important to locate driveways away from major intersections.  This 
constraint is as much for the ability to enter and exit the property as for the 
benefit of intersection safety and operations.  Exiting a driveway during peak 
hour conditions at a signalized intersection is difficult because the queue of 
standing or slow-moving vehicles may not allow a sufficient gap for entry from 
the driveway.  See Figure 3.2 for acceptable corner clearance distances.
