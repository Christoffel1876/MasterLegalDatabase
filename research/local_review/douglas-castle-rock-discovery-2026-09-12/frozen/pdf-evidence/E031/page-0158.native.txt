3. 
The following is required for the Town review/approval: 
 Depiction of utilities on stamped plans in such a way that they meet or 
exceed ASCE 38 or provide documented reasons from a licensed 
professional engineer why they do not meet or exceed Quality Level B.  
 Meeting or exceed Quality Level A for underground facilities at the 
point of a potential conflict with water mains and gravity fed systems 
including sanitary and/or stormwater facilities.  
 Test hole location symbols shown of each potential utility conflict.  
 A Test Hole Summary Report shall be provided on the plans with:  
• Test Hole Number 
• Utility Type 
• Utility Material 
• Utility Size 
• Approximate Station 
• Approximate Station Offset 
• Depth (Top) 
• Elevation (Top) 
• Cross Sectional View 
• Utility Direction 
• Surface Type 
• Pavement Thickness  
 
 The horizontal accuracy and Vertical Accuracy for QLA and QLB points 
shall be:    
• QLA:   0.2’ Horizontal & 0.1’ Vertical 
• QLB:  0.4’ Horizontal (Vertical is not applicable)
