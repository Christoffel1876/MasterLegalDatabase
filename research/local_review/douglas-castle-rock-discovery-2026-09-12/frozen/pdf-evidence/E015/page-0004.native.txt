10.3.5.1 DESC plan review fee (Detached single-family residents only) 
The DESC plan review fee shall be 65% of the DESC permit fee. 
 
 
NOTE: Additional fees may be applicable to your project based on geographic location and scope.  
For example, zoning review, excavation only, your project’s proximity to E-470 or the boundaries 
of the Cherry Creek Basin Authority may require additional fees.  Total fees for the project will be 
calculated during the plan review process and the remaining balance is due prior to permit issuance.  
If you have questions about fees please contact the Building Division.
