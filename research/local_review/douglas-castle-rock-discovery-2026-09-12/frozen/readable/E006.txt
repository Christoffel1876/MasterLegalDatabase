Environmental Health
When you eat at a restaurant or drop off your child at daycare, you want to be sure it’s a safe environment. That’s where you’ll find the environmental health experts from the Douglas County Health Department in action.
Environmental Health focuses on preventing foodborne illnesses in restaurants; preventing the spread of communicable diseases in childcare facilities, body art facilities, and water systems; and mitigating other environmental conditions that could be harmful to your health.
