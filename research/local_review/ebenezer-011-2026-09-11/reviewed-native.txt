Building Permit Fee Increase of 2.29% on July 1, 2026 
 
In 2008, the Board of County Commissioners passed a resolution authorizing the Building 
Division to annually adjust Building Permit Fee Schedule Tables 1-A, 1-B, 1-C, and 1-E based on 
the US Department of Labor/Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Price Index (CPI) for 
the Denver-Boulder-Greeley, Colorado area. Based on this year’s 2.29% increase in the 
successor regional CPI, the Building Division will adjust those fee tables accordingly, effective 
July 1, 2026. 
 
Transportation Capital Expansion Fee Increase Effective 
October 1, 2026  
The adjustment in the fees each year reflects changes in road construction costs during the 
previous year and is based on the Colorado Construction Cost Index data compiled and 
reported by the Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT). Since 1998, Larimer County 
has been collecting transportation capital expansion fees from new traffic generating 
development to be used for improvements to the road system to accommodate the increase in 
traffic generated by the new development.
