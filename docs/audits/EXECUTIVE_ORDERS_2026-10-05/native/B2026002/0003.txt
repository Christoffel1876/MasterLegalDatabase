Executive Order B 2026 002 
September 8, 2026 
Page 3 of 3 
CDHS, in consultation with the Office of Tribal and American Indian/Alaska 
Native Affairs, shall request a formal consultation with the Southern Ute Indian 
Tribe and Ute Mountain Ute Tribe to identify and discuss solutions for challenges 
the Tribes or their members face when interacting with the state competency 
system. CDHS shall incorporate this feedback into the work of the Task Force. 
Duration 
This Executive Order shall remain in effect until June 4, 2027, unless modified or 
rescinded by future Executive Order of the Governor. In all other respects, Executive Order B 
2026 001 shall remain in full force and effect as originally promulgated. 
GIVEN under my hand and the 
Executive Seal of the State of 
Colorado this eighth  
day of September, 2026.

Governor
