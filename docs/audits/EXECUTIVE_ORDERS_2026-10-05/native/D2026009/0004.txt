Declarations and Directives 
A. 
The Snyder Fire, Gold Mountain Fire, Willow Fire, and Aspen Acres Fire and 
associated impacts constitute a disaster emergency under C.R.S. § 24-33.5-701, et 
seq. My verbal orders of June 27, 28, and 29, 2026, declaring a disaster 
emergency, are hereby memorialized by this Executive Order and shall have the 
full force and effect of law as if they were contained within this Executive Order.  
B. 
My verbal orders of June 27, 28, and 29, 2026, activated the State Emergency 
Operations Plan. All State departments and agencies are authorized to take 
whatever actions may be required and requested by the Director or their delegate 
of the Office of Emergency Management pursuant to the State Emergency 
Operations Plan, including provision of appropriate staff and equipment, as 
necessary. 
C. 
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 28-3-104, this Executive Order hereby memorializes my 
verbal orders of June 27, 28, 29, 2026, authorizing employing unarmed members 
of the Colorado National Guard to support fire suppression efforts as deemed 
necessary by the Colorado Department of Public Safety for the Snyder Fire, Gold 
Mountain Fire, Willow Fire, and Aspen Acres Fire and my verbal authorization 
has the full force and effect of law as if they were contained within this Executive 
Order. 
D. 
I direct State agencies to pursue all available federal funding for response and 
recovery operations. 
E. 
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-706(4)(b), funds in the Disaster Emergency Fund 
(DEF) are insufficient to pay for fire suppression, response, consequence 
management, mitigation, and recovery efforts related to the Snyder Fire, Gold 
Mountain Fire, Willow Fire, and Aspen Acres Fire. Therefore, I order that one 
hundred three million, seven hundred seventy-two thousand, eight hundred sixty-
one dollars ($103,772,861) be transferred from the State Emergency Reserve 
Cash Fund (SERCF) to the DEF. I encumber two hundred sixty-five million, eight 
hundred five thousand, two hundred ninety dollars ($265,805,290) in the DEF to 
pay for fire suppression, response, consequence management, mitigation, and 
recovery efforts related to the Snyder Fire, Gold Mountain Fire, Willow Fire, and 
Aspen Acres Fire.
