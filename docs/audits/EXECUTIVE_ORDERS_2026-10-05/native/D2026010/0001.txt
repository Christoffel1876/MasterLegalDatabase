EXECUTIVE ORDER 
Declaring a Disaster Emergency Due to the Sheep Head Fire in Huerfano County and 
Authorizing Employment of the Colorado National Guard 
Pursuant to the authority vested in the Governor of the State of Colorado and, in 
particular, pursuant to Article IV, Section 2, of the Colorado Constitution and the relevant 
portions of the Colorado Disaster Emergency Act, C.R.S. § 24-33.5-701, et seq., I, Jared Polis, 
Governor of the State of Colorado, hereby issue this Executive Order declaring a state of disaster 
emergency due to the Sheep Head Fire in Huerfano County, enabling State and federal agencies 
to coordinate fire suppression, response, consequence management, mitigation, and recovery 
efforts.  
Further, pursuant to Article IV, Section 5 of the Colorado Constitution and C.R.S. § 28-3-
104, I authorize employing unarmed members of the Colorado National Guard for the purpose of 
supporting fire suppression and response efforts as deemed necessary by the Colorado 
Department of Public Safety for the Sheep Head Fire. 
 
Background and Purpose 
The Governor is responsible for meeting the dangers to the State and people presented by 
disasters. C.R.S. § 24-33.5-704(1). The Colorado Disaster Emergency Act defines a disaster as 
“the occurrence or imminent threat of widespread or severe damage, injury, or loss of life or 
property resulting from any natural cause or cause of human origin, including but not limited to 
fire.”  C.R.S. § 24-33.5-703(3). 
On June 29, 2026, the Sheep Head Fire started in Huerfano County near Walsenburg. On 
June 30, 2026, at approximately 2:15 PM, the Lieutenant Governor, acting as Governor, verbally 
declared a disaster emergency due to the Sheep Head Fire in Huerfano County, activated the 
State of Colorado Emergency Operations Plan, and directed the Office of Emergency 
Management (OEM) to take all necessary and appropriate State actions to assist the affected 
jurisdictions with their fire suppression, response, consequence management, mitigation, and 
recovery efforts. At the same time, the Lieutenant Governor, acting as Governor, verbally 
authorized employing unarmed members of the Colorado National Guard to respond to the 
Sheep Head Fire by providing fire suppression and response efforts. As of July 21, 2026, the 
Sheep Head Fire burned 1,322 acres in Huerfano County and is 100% contained.
