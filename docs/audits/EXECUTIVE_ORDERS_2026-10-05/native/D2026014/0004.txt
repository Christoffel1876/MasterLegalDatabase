Duration 
This Executive Order, extending Executive Order D 2026 009, shall expire thirty (30) 
days from August 21, 2026, unless extended further by Executive Order, except that the funds 
described in Section II(E) of Executive Order D 2026 009 shall remain available for the 
described purposes for seventy-two (72) months from the date of Executive Order D 2026 009.
