F. 
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-705.4(5), the Director of the OEM is authorized and 
directed to allocate the funding described in Section II(E) to the appropriate 
government agencies and nonprofit organizations and execute awards, purchase 
orders, or other mechanisms to effect the allocation of funds. The funds described 
in Section II(E) above shall remain available for this purpose until seventy-two 
(72) months from the date of this Executive Order, and any unexpended funds 
shall remain in the DEF. 
G. 
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-704(7)(a), I temporarily suspend the following 
statutory and regulatory requirements related to currently certified or licensed out-
of-state EMS providers from non-Compact states: 
a. C.R.S. § 25-3.5-202, § 25-3.5-203, and § 25-3.5-206 are suspended for 
currently certified or licensed out-of-state EMS providers from non- 
Compact states who are requested through CDPS incident command 
structure to assist in wildfire response and who are credentialed to practice 
under the co-direction of their home state medical director and a Colorado 
medical director to operate within the incident command structure. 
b. CCR 1015-3, Chapter 2 is suspended for currently certified or licensed 
out-of-state Emergency Medical Service (EMS) providers from non- 
Compact states who are requested through CDPS incident command 
structure to assist in wildfire response and providers who are credentialed 
to practice under the co-direction of their home state medical director and 
a Colorado medical director operating within the incident command 
structure.
