On August 7, 2026, at approximately 5:23 PM, I verbally declared a disaster emergency 
due to the 310 Fire in Garfield County, activated the State of Colorado Emergency Operations 
Plan, and directed the Office of Emergency Management (OEM) to take all necessary and 
appropriate State actions to assist the affected jurisdictions with their fire suppression, response, 
consequence management, mitigation, and recovery efforts. At the same time, I verbally 
authorized employing unarmed members of the Colorado National Guard to respond to the 310 
Fire by providing fire suppression and response efforts. As of August 19, 2026, the 310 Fire 
burned 603 acres in Garfield County and is 100% contained. 
 
Declarations and Directives 
A. 
The 310 Fire and associated impacts constitute a disaster emergency under C.R.S. 
§ 24-33.5-701, et seq. My verbal order of August 7, 2026, declaring a disaster 
emergency, is hereby memorialized by this Executive Order and shall have the 
full force and effect of law as if it were contained within this Executive Order. 
B. 
My verbal order of August 7, 2026, activated the State Emergency Operations 
Plan. All State departments and agencies are authorized to take whatever actions 
may be required and requested by the Director or their delegate of OEM pursuant 
to the State Emergency Operations Plan, including provision of appropriate staff 
and equipment, as necessary. 
C. 
Further, pursuant to C.R.S. § 28-3-104, this Executive Order hereby memorializes 
my verbal order of August 7, 2026, authorizing employing unarmed members of 
the Colorado National Guard to support fire suppression and response efforts as 
deemed necessary by the CDPS for the 310 Fire, and my verbal authorization has 
the full force and effect of law as if it were contained within this Executive Order. 
D. 
I direct State agencies to pursue all available federal funding for response and 
recovery operations. 
E. 
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-706(4)(b), funds in the Disaster Emergency Fund 
(DEF) are insufficient to pay for fire suppression, response, consequence 
management, mitigation, and recovery efforts related to the 310 Fire. Therefore, I 
order that one million, one hundred ninety-three dollars ($1,193,000) be 
transferred from the State Emergency Reserve Cash Fund (SERCF) to the DEF. I
