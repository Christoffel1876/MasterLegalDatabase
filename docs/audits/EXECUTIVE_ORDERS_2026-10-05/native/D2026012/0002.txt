Declarations and Directives 
A. 
The Green Ridge Fire and associated impacts constitute a disaster emergency 
under C.R.S. § 24-33.5-701, et seq. My verbal order of July 12, 2026, declaring a 
disaster emergency, is hereby memorialized by this Executive Order and shall 
have the full force and effect of law as if it were contained within this Executive 
Order. 
B. 
My verbal order of July 12, 2026, activated the State Emergency Operations Plan. 
All State departments and agencies are authorized to take whatever actions may 
be required and requested by the Director of OEM or their delegate pursuant to 
the State Emergency Operations Plan, including provision of appropriate staff and 
equipment, as necessary. 
C. 
Further, pursuant to C.R.S. § 28-3-104, this Executive Order hereby memorializes 
the my verbal order of July 12, 2026, authorizing employing unarmed members of 
the Colorado National Guard to support fire suppression and response efforts as 
deemed necessary by CDPS for the Green Ridge Fire, and my verbal 
authorization has the full force and effect of law as if it were contained within this 
Executive Order. 
D. 
I direct State agencies to pursue all available federal funding for response and 
recovery operations. 
E. 
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-33.5-706(4)(b), funds in the Disaster Emergency Fund 
(DEF) are insufficient to pay for fire suppression, response, consequence 
management, mitigation, and recovery efforts related to the Green Ridge Fire. 
Therefore, I order that one million, eight hundred eighty-one thousand dollars 
($1,881,000) be transferred from the State Emergency Reserve Cash Fund 
(SERCF) to the DEF. I encumber one million, eight hundred eighty-one thousand 
dollars ($1,881,000) in the DEF to pay for fire suppression, response, 
consequence management, mitigation, and recovery efforts related to the Green 
Ridge Fire.
