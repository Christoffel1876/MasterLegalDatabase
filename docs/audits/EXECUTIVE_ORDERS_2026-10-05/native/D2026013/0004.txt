iii. 
Staffing 
1. Establish productivity benchmarks for permitting teams and 
staff and use performance data to inform staff recognition, 
workload management, and professional development. 
2. Ensure biannual permitting staff trainings are in place to 
support ongoing, agile learning on new regulations, 
technology systems, and other changes. 
iv. 
Regulatory Structure 
1. Develop recommendations for permit streamlining by 
identifying a few initial opportunities where targeted 
changes to laws, regulations, or policies governing the 
permit review process could improve efficiency and clarity 
while maintaining the integrity and purpose of permit 
reviews. Examples include, but are not limited to, enabling 
parallel reviews or standardizing internal policies and 
processes. The policies and processes shall acknowledge 
Tribal sovereignty for projects that cross State-Tribal 
jurisdictions, including notification to and/or approval from 
the applicable Tribal governments, as required by law, prior 
to project commencement. 
v. 
Technology 
1. Evaluate the costs and feasibility of technology that would 
allow key permitting systems to be inter-operable and 
prepare recommendations for technology development in 
2027 and beyond. 
2. For APCD and WQCD, make meaningful progress to 
digitize remaining paper permit systems, with a goal of 
moving at least 50% of permit volume from paper to digital 
by April 2027. For ECMC and HMWMD, make best 
efforts to further digitize as possible with existing 
resources. 
C. Other 
1. In accomplishing the goals and objectives of this Executive Order, the 
State Agencies and Divisions shall collaborate as necessary with other 
State departments, agencies, and offices, and external stakeholders for 
feedback on how State government can best serve Coloradans and 
Strategic Industries engaging with State permit systems.
