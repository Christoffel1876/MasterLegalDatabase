The Forest Service set aside $500,000 for the grant program and worked with 
stakeholders and the agency’s field offices to circulate information about the program. 
Staff also created a grant application that was posted on the agency’s website. As the 
grant program had limited funding, no deadline was set. When staff received the 
applications, they reviewed them and approved those that fit the purpose of the 
program. The funds were then released on a reimbursement basis.

The Forest Service awarded $498,600 in grants to 12 projects supporting biomass 
utilization throughout the state. The awards ranged from $5,000 to $75,000 and 
averaged about $41,550.
