TRAINING LICENSE  
A training license may be issued to any person who is not otherwise licensed to practice 
medicine in Colorado and who is serving in an approved internship, residency or 
fellowship in a hospital.75 The holder of a training license may practice medicine only 
under the supervision of a fully licensed physician, 76 may not delegate medical 
functions to a person who is not a licensed physician, and may not supervise physician 
assistants.77 An individual may not hold a training license for more than six years.78
