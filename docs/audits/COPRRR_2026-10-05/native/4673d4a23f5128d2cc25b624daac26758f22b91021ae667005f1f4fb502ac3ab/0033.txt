Disciplinary Activity 
 
The tenth sunset criterion requires COPRRR to examine whether complaint, 
investigation and disciplinary procedures adequately protect the public and whether 
final dispositions of complaints are in the public interest or self-serving to the 
profession or regulated entity. 
 
In part, COPRRR utilizes this section of the report to evaluate the program according to 
this criterion.  
 
If the Office receives a complaint that demonstrates a violation of the State Act, 
Federal Act or the Commission’s rules, the Division Director can take disciplinary action 
against the licensee.  Table 11 illustrates, for the fiscal years indicated, the total 
number and types of final actions taken. 
 
 
As the data in Table 11 indicate, only 10 of the 41 (24.4 percent) complaints reported 
in Table 10 resulted in disciplinary action. 
 
The single revocation was against a promoter. 
 
The two cases that were “combined with other cases” both involved a single promoter, 
so they were combined into a single case. 
 
Of the three stipulations, two involved participants and one involved a corner. 
 
The four cases in which a license was denied involved two participants and two corners.
