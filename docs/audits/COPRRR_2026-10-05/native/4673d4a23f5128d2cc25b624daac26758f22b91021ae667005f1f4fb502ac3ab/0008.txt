Sunset Process 
 
Regulatory programs scheduled for sunset review receive a comprehensive analysis. The 
review includes a thorough dialogue with agency officials, representatives of the 
regulated profession and other stakeholders. Anyone can submit input on any upcoming 
sunrise or sunset review on COPRRR’s website at coprrr.colorado.gov. 
 
The functions of the Director of the Division of Professions and Occupations (Director 
and Division, respectively), the Office of Combative Sports (Office Director and Office, 
respectively) and the Colorado Combative Sports Commission (Commission), as 
enumerated in Article 110 of Title 12, Colorado Revised Statutes (C.R.S.), shall 
terminate on September 1, 2026, unless continued by the General Assembly. During the 
year prior to this date, it is the duty of COPRRR to conduct an analysis and evaluation 
of the Colorado Professional Boxing Safety Act (State Act), the Director, the Office 
Director and the Commission pursuant to section 24-34-104, C.R.S.
