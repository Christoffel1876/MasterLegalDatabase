 Recommendation - Food safety training and support. 
o Educate institutional buyers about tradeoffs and risk management and 
mitigation as it relates to food safety. 
o Explore opportunities for group good agricultural practices certification

safety certification requirements and potential exceptions.

• Recommendation – Strengthen interagency coordination on food systems 
issues. 
o Support efforts to promote coordination between state agencies that 
administer food and agriculture programs. 
o Make data on state programs more readily available to agency and 
community partners. 
o Actions taken: 
 The Advisory Council compiled and disseminated quarterly updates 
from state agencies on food systems related policies, programs, 
funding opportunities, and events. These reports represent the first 
cross-agency compilation of food systems efforts and are shared at 
public quarterly meetings and via Advisory Council newsletters. 
 Colorado State University Extension convened a working group of 
state agencies administering food and agriculture programs in 
Colorado. This group facilitated interagency dialogue and produced 
a working inventory of state programs, establishing a foundation 
for improved alignment and resource sharing.
