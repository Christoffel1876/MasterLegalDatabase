There are also financial-related violations such as:106 
 
• Failure by the broker to promptly give a client’s deposit money to their 
brokerage firm; 
• Failing to account for or to remit, within a reasonable time, any money coming 
into the licensee’s possession that belongs to others, and failing to keep records 
related to the money; 
• Converting funds of others, diverting funds of others without proper 
authorization, commingling funds of others with the broker’s own funds, or 
failing to keep the funds of others in an escrow or a trust account; and 
• Claiming, arranging for, or taking any secret or undisclosed amount of 
compensation, commission, or profit or failing to reveal to the licensee’s 
principal or employer the full amount of the licensee’s compensation. 
 
The Commission can impose an administrative fine up to $2,500 for each separate 
offense, issue letters of admonition, censure a licensee, place the licensee on 
probation, and temporarily suspend a license for violations.107 All administrative fines 
are credited directly into the Division’s cash fund.108 The Commission also has the 
ability to apply to a court for an injunction to cease actions that are violations.109 
 
While the Act states that the Commission may permanently revoke a license,110 it also 
states that licensees can reapply after one year of the revocation.111
