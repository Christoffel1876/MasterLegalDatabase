The Division is part of the Department of Regulatory Agencies and acts on behalf of the 
Commission when directed by the Commission. 153 It hires attorneys, deputies, 
investigators, clerks, and assistants to carry out these duties. 154 With regards to 
subdivision registrants, the Division currently handles most duties including 
registration.
