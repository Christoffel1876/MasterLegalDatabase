Recommendation 5 — Make a technical amendment to the Act. 
 
The Act contains outdated language that should be revised to reflect current 
terminology and administrative practices. Therefore, the General Assembly should 
amend the Act to address the following technical issue:  
 
• Amend the Act to make it gender neutral by replacing terms such as “him”, 
“her”, “he”, and “she” with gender-neutral terms.
