CPW’s boating safety program is funded through two primary sources: the fees CPW 
collects for registering vessels and a grant awarded by the U.S. Coast Guard under the 
Federal Recreational Boating Safety (RBS) Grant Program.  The Program comprises only 
a portion of the duties conducted by CPW staff related to boating safety.
