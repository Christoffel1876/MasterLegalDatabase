Oftentimes, CPW officers may simply issue a warning if a vessel is not registered.  This 
is particularly true if there are both safety and registration violations.  Since the 
registration violation is viewed as administrative in nature, greater emphasis is placed 
on the safety issues. 
 
Regardless, citations for registration violations entail a fine of $100.
