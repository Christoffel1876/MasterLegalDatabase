As such, bail bonding agents provide an important service for defendants, and in order 
to continue to provide these services with regulatory safeguards to protect consumers 
from harm, the General Assembly should continue the regulation of bail bonding agents 
for 13 years, until 2039.  Thirteen years is justified because the program is functioning 
effectively and this sunset report makes no additional recommendations.
