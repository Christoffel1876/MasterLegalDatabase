Methodology 
 
As part of this review, COPRRR staff interviewed Division of Insurance staff, cash-bonding 
and professional cash-bail agent practitioners, and officials with state and national 
professional associations; and reviewed Colorado statutes and rules. 
 
The major contacts made during this review include, but are not limited to:  
 
• Division of Insurance  
• Colorado Bar Association 
• Colorado Trial Lawyers Association 
• Dennis Blackwell Bail Bonds 
• Express Bail Bonds 
• Bail City Bail Bonds
