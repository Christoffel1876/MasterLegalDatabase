The ICC and NFPA offer credentials for inspectors. The Inspector II classification 
requires one to have an ICC Fire Inspector II or NFPA Fire Inspector II credential. If a 
candidate does not have either of these credentials, they must demonstrate that they 
have the requisite skills and knowledge specified in NFPA 1031.132 NFPA 1031 is a widely 
recognized standard that specified necessary requirements, knowledge, and skills for 
individuals who conduct fire inspections of buildings.133
