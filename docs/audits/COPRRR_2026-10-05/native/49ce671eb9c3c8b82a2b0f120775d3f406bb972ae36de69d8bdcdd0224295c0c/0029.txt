The Division can also provide inspections for localities on a contractual or job-by-job 
basis. 123 The locality reimburses the Division for such services. 124 The number of 
required inspections depends on the size of the project. Projects under 50,000 square 
feet may require up to five site visits. For projects between 50,001 and 100,000 square 
feet, up to 10 site visits are conducted. For larger projects, up to five visits take place. 
Additional visits can be made for each additional 100,000 square feet.125 Inspections 
take place until the installation of a system is complete. 
 
During an inspection, the inspector typically performs a hydrostatic test of the system, 
conducts operational tests to examine waterflow or system pressure, and examines the 
control valve’s tamper switches. Inspectors also check system coverage to see if there 
are any blocked sprinkler heads.
