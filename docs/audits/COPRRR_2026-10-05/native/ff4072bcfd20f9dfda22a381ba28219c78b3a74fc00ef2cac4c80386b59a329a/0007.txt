Sunset Process 
 
Regulatory programs scheduled for sunset review receive a comprehensive analysis. The 
review includes a thorough dialogue with agency officials, representatives of the 
regulated profession and other stakeholders. Anyone can submit input on any upcoming 
sunrise or sunset review on COPRRR’s website at coprrr.colorado.gov. 
 
The Colorado Licensing of Controlled Substances Act (Act) and the functions of the 
Behavioral Health Administration (BHA), located in the Department of Human Services, 
as enumerated in Article Part 2 of Article 80 of Title 27, Colorado Revised Statutes 
(C.R.S.), shall terminate on September 1, 2026, unless continued by the General 
Assembly. During the year prior to this date, it is the duty of COPRRR to conduct an 
analysis and evaluation of the Act pursuant to section 24-34-104, C.R.S.
