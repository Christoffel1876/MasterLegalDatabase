This can help a detective determine whether there are any other questions that need 
to be resolved, any additional interviews that need to be completed, and follow up on

The agency can choose to use public outreach for help on cold cases. The media can be 
used to request information, especially in homicides with limited evidence. This might 
create new leads from members of the public, or other agencies that might have 
information related to the case. Jurisdictions such as Arapahoe County, 13 
Westminster, 14 Glendale, 15 and Colorado Springs 16 post active cold cases on their 
websites, for example. 
 
A traditional method of solving cold cases utilizes a combination of fingerprinting 
technology and DNA analysis. 17 Biological evidence can be analyzed to produce a 
reliable DNA profile, which can be compared to fingerprint databases or other 
databases available to law enforcement in order to find a potential match.  
 
An emerging technique for solving cold cases involves using forensic investigative 
genetic genealogy,18 which compares DNA evidence to public genealogy databases.19 
This can be used to find relatives of perpetrators or victims. It is a relatively new 
method that first emerged in 2018.20
