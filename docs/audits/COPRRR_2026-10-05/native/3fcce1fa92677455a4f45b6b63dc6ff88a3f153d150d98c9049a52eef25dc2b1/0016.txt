There are currently 2,055 cases listed on the database.44 Of all these cases, 1,975 are 
listed as “open” and seven are listed as “other” cases. “Other” cases also remain 
unsolved but are listed separately because the reporting agency has not made a decision 
on whether it was a homicide, so they are reported as “other.” The remaining 73 cases 
are listed as “solved.” Any member of the public may access the database and search 
for cases based on queries such as: 
 
• Years; 
• Agency; 
• Case status; 
• Identifying marks; 
• Names or aliases of victims; 
• Tribal affiliations of victims; and 
• Physical details of victims such as age, gender, race, height, weight, hair and 
eye color. 
 
 
 
44 Colorado Bureau of Investigation. Cold Case Files. Retrieved September 2, 2025, from
