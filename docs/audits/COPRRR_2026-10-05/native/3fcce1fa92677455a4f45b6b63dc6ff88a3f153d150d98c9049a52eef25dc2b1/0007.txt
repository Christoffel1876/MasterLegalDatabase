Sunset Process 
 
Regulatory programs scheduled for sunset review receive a comprehensive analysis. The 
review includes a thorough dialogue with agency officials, and other stakeholders. 
Anyone can submit input on any upcoming sunrise or sunset review on COPRRR’s website 
at coprrr.colorado.gov. 
 
The functions of the Cold Case Task Force (Task Force) and the Colorado Bureau of 
Investigation (CBI), as enumerated in Section 109 of Article 33.5 of Title 24, Colorado 
Revised Statutes (C.R.S.), shall terminate on September 1, 2026, unless continued by 
the General Assembly. During the year prior to this date, it is the duty of COPRRR to 
conduct an analysis and evaluation of the Task Force pursuant to section 24-34-104, 
C.R.S.
