There are several factors that are considered when determining the type of formal 
discipline imposed on practitioners, such as the nature of the violation, harm to the 
public, substandard practice and whether the practitioner has received formal 
discipline in the past.  
 
Additionally, the Director and boards often have the authority to impose fines on 
practitioners for violations of statutes or applicable rules.
