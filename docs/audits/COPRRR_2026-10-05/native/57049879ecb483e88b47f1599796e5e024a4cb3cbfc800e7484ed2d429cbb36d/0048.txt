Appendix A — Regulated Practitioners and Businesses 
in the Division of Professions & Occupations 
 
The Division of Professions and Occupations (Division) in the Department of Regulatory 
Agencies houses and provides administrative support to licensing programs that regulate 
over 60 types of practitioners and businesses, including those in health care and other 
fields. The following lists enumerate the various types of practitioners and businesses 
regulated by programs located in the Division.
