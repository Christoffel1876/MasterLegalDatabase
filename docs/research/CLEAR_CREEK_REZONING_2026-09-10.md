# Clear Creek: provisional standard rezoning comparison

The comparable process is a **standard amendment to the Official Zone District Map under Section 19**, for an owner of an existing parcel in unincorporated Clear Creek County. It is suitable for comparison only if the proposed use actually requires a map amendment. The preapplication meeting determines that question. A project that is already permitted by its zoning may not need this process; a hardship request, Planned Development rezoning, M-1-to-M-2-only downzoning, subdivision, or special-use proposal needs its own analysis. Section 19 is not itself permission to construct or operate a development. [Section 19, PDF pp. 1–2, printed pp. 19-1–19-2](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/566); [jurisdiction, Section 1 §104, PDF p. 4](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/551).

The sequence supported by the selected text is: required preapplication meeting; optional sketch review or direct final-plan application; application and completeness screening; referrals and notices; Planning Commission recommendation; BOCC hearing and decision; final-document requirements and recording. A companion site-specific development plan is encouraged but is not required by §1903 alone. Development Review under Section 20, building/site-development approvals, and other laws are **separate dependencies excluded from this sample**, not assumed unnecessary. No parcel, district pair, acreage, use, or development dimensions have been supplied.

## Version and source limits

The 194-page whole compilation in the pilot (`CC-ZONING-927`, SHA-256 `c851cabd2009c78ca7f94553121302923c2fa7bc0c8e151e50ed2f3a4f6f09c3`) has September 2020 revision markers. Its Section 19 is dated August 29, 2011. The **currently linked, freshly downloaded Section 19 PDF 566** is dated the same day, and each of its six normalized page texts exactly matches whole-compilation PDF pages 131–136. This supports the selected published procedure; it does not prove no later amendment exists. The current [zoning catalog](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/288/Zoning-Regulations) separately links the sections.

The fresh Section 1 PDF 551 is revised December 7, 2021 and adds R-21-91 concerning short-term-rental provisions in Sections 12 and 23. The whole 2020 compilation therefore cannot be treated as the complete current code. Separately preserved [Ordinance 19](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/15243/2024-12-17-Ord-19-CCC-STR-Regulations-FINAL_publication), visually inspected at PDF pages 1, 12, and 13, was adopted December 17, 2024. Its §10.1 preserves pre-existing STR permits under former zoning §1207 until expiry; §13 states immediate effectiveness on second-reading adoption. This is a concrete later-law example concerning STR licensing, **not a finding that rezoning Section 19 changed**. The complete 2021–2026 amendment/adoption history has not been reconciled.

The [Rezoning Process Guide and form, PDF 298](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentView.aspx?DID=298&DL=1) is **official guidance and a form**, not an independently established legislative enactment. It reproduces the Section 19 procedure and is linked from the current [Process Guides page](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/125/Process-Guides). It is undated in the inspected document and contains old browser/submission instructions. The current Planning page links a GovBuilt application portal; this study did not submit an application or verify the operative filing channel.

All observations remain **provisional pending source-version, later-amendment, and actual-parcel review**. The exact evidence, original archive paths, hashes, page numbers, and NFKC-plus-whitespace-normalized excerpts are in [combined evidence study](../../research/land_use/rezoning-2026-09-10.json).

## Fee evidence needs reconciliation

The [October 4, 2022 fee schedule](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/13479/Fee-Schedule-adopted-Oct-2022), PDF page 1, lists straight-rezoning sketch **$1,500**, final **$1,500**, combined sketch/final **$2,000**, and combined sketch/final with Development Review **$2,500**. The duplicated dollar sign on its “Final” row is present in the original. The old form on guide PDF page 9 simply says “Application Fee is $2000.” Those statements do not establish which charge applies when skipping sketch; this study does not choose or add them.

The current [Fees catalog](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/1002/Fees) links a [February 18, 2020 schedule](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/7347/final-approved-fee-schedule?bidId=), which has the same rezoning rows but lacks the 2022 preapplication entries. The [Planning Fee Schedule page](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/860/Fee-Schedule) still describes fees lasting through December 2016. Publication on an official site is established; a complete current fee adoption chain is not.

The 2022 schedule PDF page 7 says appropriate fees must be paid for completeness and restricts reductions/waivers to BOCC-approved circumstances described there, including qualifying intergovernmental reciprocity and certain withdrawn applications. Its page 5 addresses referral/professional and recording fees. [Section 1, §113, PDF page 7](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/551) separately provides for referral-agency fees and outside costs: estimated advances, additional-cost payment within ten days after notice, suspension for nonpayment, and deemed withdrawal after thirty days. These are conditional costs beyond the listed base fee, not included in a computed project total.
