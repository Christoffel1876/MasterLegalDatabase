# Standard rezoning: first county comparison

This study compares a private owner's application to change the standard zoning
district of an existing parcel in **unincorporated Jefferson or Clear Creek County**.
It makes the published steps, selected submissions, fees, timing, and exceptions
inspectable. It does not determine whether a particular parcel needs rezoning.

The evidence contains **40 selected research observations across 25 preserved
official sources**. Twenty observations per county are a sampling choice, not a
burden count, completeness measure, or ranking. Applicant duties and government
procedures have distinct actors. Every observation remains provisional until the
applicable amendment history, incorporated standards, and parcel facts are checked.

## Comparable process

| Stage | Jefferson | Clear Creek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before filing | Optional preliminary application review; community meeting required unless a written waiver is granted. | Required preapplication meeting; sketch review is optional, with a direct final-plan route described. |
| Application | Electronic Section 9 package; completeness precedes referral. | Section 19 package; incomplete submissions are not processed. The text describes initial and distribution copies, but the current filing channel needs confirmation. |
| Selected evidence | Application, proposal letter, ownership evidence, water, wastewater, fire service, environmental material. | Ownership evidence, map, written explanation, mineral-estate notice certification, survey, utility and site-study evidence. |
| Public review | Referrals and notices, then Planning Commission recommendation and Board of County Commissioners decision. | Referrals and notices, then Planning Commission recommendation and Board of County Commissioners decision. |
| After approval | Standard-zone resolution is to be recorded within seven calendar days after approval. | Final-document and recording requirements remain; approval does not end every obligation. |

Sources: Jefferson Zoning Resolution [Sections 4–6, PDF pp. 23–30](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/1828#page=23)
and [Section 9, PDF pp. 46–54](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/1828#page=46);
Clear Creek [Section 19, PDF pp. 1–6](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/566).

Both counties preserve discretion over submission requirements. Jefferson allows
reuse of previously approved documents that remain applicable, comply with current
County regulations, and concern consistent development circumstances, and waiver of documents that
would not materially aid review; additional documents can also be requested.
Clear Creek's Planning Director may waive information irrelevant to approval
criteria or already supplied with a companion application. These information
waivers do not establish a general fee waiver. See Jefferson
[§9.B, PDF p. 46](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/1828#page=46) and Clear Creek
[§1904 introduction, PDF p. 3](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/566#page=3).

## Selected published fees

| Item | Jefferson | Clear Creek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard rezoning application | $400 × square root of acreage, with a $900 minimum. | Schedules list sketch $1,500; final $1,500; combined sketch/final $2,000. The applicable route and operative schedule need confirmation. |
| Preliminary meeting/review | Optional planning preliminary review $350, plus listed Public Health preliminary review $275. The policy permits credit of the preliminary development application fee toward formal submission within one year; a separate Public Health credit is not established. | The 2022 schedule lists a $200 preapplication meeting fee, credited toward application fees if formal application is made within one year of the meeting. The older schedule linked by the current catalog does not have that entry. |
| Community meeting / hearings | Community meeting $75 when used; combined Planning Commission/Board hearing fee $400 when scheduled. | No separate comparable charge is established by this sample; that does not mean none applies. |
| Conditional additional costs | Public Health rezoning review $350 if charged on referral; postage/materials, studies, further referrals, and other charges may apply. | Referral-agency, outside-professional, and recording costs may apply. The $2,500 combined route includes Development Review, outside this study's main scope. |

Sources: Jefferson [2026 fee table, PDF p. 1](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/15930/#page=1),
[fee policy, PDF pp. 1–2](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/320/Development-Application-and-Permit-Fees-Policy-PDF),
[Public Health 2026 schedule](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/51106/),
and [§9.C.2, PDF p. 48](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/1828#page=48);
Clear Creek [2022 schedule, PDF pp. 1, 4–5, 7](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/13479/Fee-Schedule-adopted-Oct-2022),
[2020 schedule](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/7347/final-approved-fee-schedule?bidId=),
and [Section 1, §113, PDF p. 7](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/551#page=7).

**Do not add these rows into a universal project price.** Jefferson's policy
contains credits, qualifying affordable-housing treatment, discretionary waivers,
and violation-correction charges. Clear Creek's fee schedules contain conditions
and waiver provisions. Its current [Fees catalog](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/1002/Fees)
links the 2020 schedule, a separate official schedule says effective October 4,
2022, and the [older guide/form, PDF p. 9](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentView.aspx?DID=298&DL=1#page=9)
gives an undifferentiated $2,000 application amount. The two schedules agree on the
listed rezoning rows but differ elsewhere. Selecting one as fully current would
hide a material source gap.

## Deadlines versus estimates

| Published timing | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| Jefferson: arrange community meeting at least 21 calendar days ahead; specified notices/signs at least 14 days ahead. | Preparatory requirements; meeting waivers and notice rules must be preserved. |
| Jefferson: respond to first-referral comments within 180 calendar days. | Withdrawal threshold, with good-cause extensions. A shorter response interval supports the example hearing schedule. |
| Jefferson: estimated 106 calendar days. | First referral to public-hearing phase, conditional on completeness and the described intervals; Section 6.A permits time-frame waivers. |
| Jefferson guide: 5–6 months. | Staff goal including preapplication, with different boundaries; neither an observed average nor a guaranteed deadline. |
| Clear Creek: 21 calendar days for referral-agency comments before Planning Commission hearing. | Agency comment opportunity, not total processing duration. |
| Clear Creek: newspaper notice and applicant signs at least 14 calendar days before Planning Commission; separate newspaper notice at least 14 days before Board hearing. | Hearing notice requirements, with different responsible actors. |

Sources: Jefferson [Sections 3–6, PDF pp. 20–29](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/1828#page=20)
and [guide, PDF p. 4](https://www.jeffco.us/DocumentCenter/View/9504#page=4);
Clear Creek [§1903.3, PDF pp. 2–3](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/566#page=2).
No comparable overall Clear Creek completion time or empirical county processing
average was established. Different clocks must not become a speed ranking.

## Currency and coverage

Jefferson's full zoning PDF and [current catalog](https://www.jeffco.us/2460/Zoning-Resolution)
agree on adoption March 10, 2026 and effectiveness July 1, 2026. That agreement is
stronger than a download date, but it is not a complete later-amendment review.
The captured [ULUC project page](https://www.jeffco.us/5234/Unified-Land-Use-Code-ULUC)
describes a draft process; this study does not substitute it for adopted zoning.

Clear Creek's whole compilation carries 2020 revision markers. The separately
linked Section 19 matches its rezoning pages, while separately linked Section 1
has a December 2021 revision and a preserved STR ordinance was adopted in December
2024. Those later sources show that the whole compilation is not a complete
current-code substitute; they do not establish that Section 19 itself changed.
See the [section catalog](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/288/Zoning-Regulations),
[Section 1](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/551), and
[Ordinance 19](https://www.clearcreekcounty.us/DocumentCenter/View/15243/2024-12-17-Ord-19-CCC-STR-Regulations-FINAL_publication).

This sample excludes incorporated municipalities, special districts' independent
requirements, planned development, special use, subdivision, and later building
or development approvals. Water, wastewater, access, fire, environmental, state,
and federal requirements remain possible dependencies. No parcel/use/district
pair or acreage has been supplied. A missing observation is **not evidence that
a requirement is absent**.
